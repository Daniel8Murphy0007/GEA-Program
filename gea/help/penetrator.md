# Completion - the ESP wellhead penetrator

> The pressure-sealed passage that carries an ESP's power through the tubing hanger and the wellhead: the penetrators the program knows with the ratings their maker's documents print, each well's installation record, and the well's ESP drive, motor and site checked against those ratings one line at a time.

**The command**: the Completion tab. For each well, **Record the installation** (the operator role) takes the penetrator (a catalogue entry, or a fleet tool of the wellhead-penetrator class with its datasheet), the date, the installer and whether the maker certified them, the motor (PMM or induction), the gauge distance measured on the job, the insulation reading of each conductor with the test voltage, and the void test (pressure, minutes, held); **Declare the drive** takes the ESP drive's maximum output voltage and current and its frequency limits, the motor nameplate, the wellhead's hazardous-area classification, gas group and temperature class, and the site's ambient range, with their source. The declarations sheet takes both (`[penetrators]`, `[esp_drives]`). From a terminal: `gea penetrator` lists the penetrators and the PMM safety note; `gea penetrator --workspace C:\site --well W` prints the well's check; `--remove install` or `--remove drive`.

**What it writes**: `completion/esp.json` in the workspace, each declaration, replacement and removal a line in the audit log. The ratings ship with the program (`reference/penetrator_ratings.json`): each figure with the maker's document, its revision and the section it is printed in; none of the documents' procedure text.

**The check**: each line WITHIN, OUTSIDE, NOT RATED (the document prints no figure), NOT DECLARED (the site has not given one) or NOT STATED (the document does not say). Voltage and current: the drive's maximum output and the motor nameplate against the maximum voltage and the full-load current. Frequency: the drive's limits inside the rated range. Hazardous area: a Division 1 wellhead needs a Division 1 rating, a Division 2 wellhead either; the zones likewise; the gas group and temperature class against the rating. Ambient and altitude (the well's declared elevation). The job: the certified installer where the maker requires one, the motor type, the gauge distance, the three insulation readings (against the site's own acceptance minimum when one is declared, with its source), and the void test held at or below the rated working pressure. The well is WITHIN only when every line is.

**Permanent magnet motors**: a PMM's rotor is always magnetised, so fluid turning the pump makes the motor a generator - voltage on the cable with the drive off, while running in and pulling, after a shutdown as fluid falls back, in an intervention or a kick. The tab carries the safety note and its sources; a PMM well's check carries it too.

**The number to check**: a rating against the maker's document named beside it; the altitude against the well's elevation in feet times 0.3048.

**What this page will not call a measurement**: every figure on it - the ratings are the maker's, the installation record and the drive are the operator's declarations; the program measures none of them. An insulation acceptance minimum is the site's, never the program's.
