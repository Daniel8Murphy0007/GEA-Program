# Well collision checks

> Every well scanned against every other - a well not yet drilled along its plan, a well being drilled along its survey and then its plan: at each point of one well, the closest point of the other; how far apart they are against how sure their positions are, by the ISCWSA separation factor; the travelling cylinder; each well's position uncertainty by its survey program's ISCWSA / OWSG tool models, with the Earth's field at the well from WMM2025. The Volve template is the worked example.

**The command**: the **Collision** tab: the lowest separation factor and where it is, each pair's separation factor along the reference well against the levels; the travelling cylinder - looking down one well, each neighbour within 10 to 250 m at its distance and direction (clockwise from the high side, or from north while the reference is within 5 degrees of vertical); every pair's minimum; each well's field, tools and uncertainty at TD; the rule and the hole sizes (the operator role); **Write the report**. The scene **Worked example: the Volve template** shows the same check on three public Volve wells. From a terminal: `gea collision --workspace C:\site` prints it; `--write` writes the report; `--scene volve` the worked example; `--k 3.5 --surface-margin 0.3 --sigma-pa 0.5 --source "company anti-collision standard"` keeps a rule; `--well W --hole 12.25` sets a well's hole size; `--field 31.42,-104.10,2026-10-20` prints the WMM2025 field at a place and date.

**What a well needs**: a surface position, a reference elevation and a directional survey or a plan (Setup, the declarations sheet's `[wells]` and `[surveys]`, or the Plan vs actual tab). A well without them is listed as not checked, with why. A well with a plan and no survey is checked along its plan, weighed as if surveyed every 30 m by its program's tools; a well surveyed short of its plan's TD is checked along its survey and then its plan. Its stations are read as the 3D tab reads them, in the pad's frame; the field is WMM2025 at the well for the date its drilling record starts (today when it has none).

**The uncertainty**: each well's position covariance at every station from the tool models of its survey program (the Plan vs actual tab; MWD+SRGM, the ISCWSA MWD Rev 5.11 standard, unless declared): the sensor biases and scale factors, the magnetometer and declination terms, misalignment, sag, the depth and course-length terms, propagated as the model says - random sources station to station, systematic along a run, well-wide along the well, global across wells. The Rev 4 model is checked against all 112 rows of the ISCWSA's own Rev 4 example, and the Rev 5.11 model against all 144 rows of its Rev 5 example.

**The rule**: SF = (D - R_r - R_o - S_m) / (k x sqrt(sigma_S^2 + sigma_pa^2)): D the distance to the closest point of the other well, R each hole's radius, S_m the surface margin (0.3 m), sigma_pa the project-ahead uncertainty (0.5 m), k the confidence factor (3.5), and sigma_S the uncertainty of the distance along the line between the two points - each well's covariance projected on that line, with the global errors both wells share taken out (they move both wells together; a global error only one well's model carries stays in). The levels are the ISCWSA's: reported below 4.0, the approach limit below 1.5, revise the plan below 1.25, below 1.0 only by the operator's dispensation. A company standard with another k or margin is kept with its source and printed on the report.

**What it writes**: the rule and the hole sizes in `wellpath\collision.json` (audit line `collision.settings`), the audit line `report.collision`, and the reports `reports\collision.*` (`reports\collision_volve.*` for the worked example; PDF, HTML, markdown, JSON).

**The number to check**: the distance between two wells at one depth against the 3D tab's paths; a well's ellipse at TD against the survey company's own uncertainty listing for the same tool; the declination against the survey's header (or NOAA's calculator for the same place and date).

**What this page will not call a measurement**: the wells' true positions - an uncertainty is what the survey tool's published error model allows, not a measured miss. A probability of collision - the separation factor is a rule's ratio, and the operator's standard says what it permits. A tool's accuracy beyond its model - each well is weighed by the tool models its survey program names; gyro and axial-interference-corrected models are not carried. A plan's position - a well not yet drilled is checked where its plan says it will be. The hole size when it is not declared (8-1/2 in by default), and the casing beyond it.

**Sources**: the ISCWSA error model (Williamson, SPE-67616-PA, 2000) and its Rev 4 example; the separation rule (Sawaryn et al., SPE Drilling & Completion 34(1), 2019) and the ISCWSA eBook's separation levels; the World Magnetic Model 2025 (NOAA NCEI and the British Geological Survey, public domain), its coefficients shipped as published in `gea\reference\WMM2025.COF`.
