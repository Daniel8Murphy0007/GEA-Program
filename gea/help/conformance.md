# Conformity, in the standards' words

> The certificate register says, item by item, what each calibration certificate carries of what ISO/IEC 17025:2017 asks; the drift report's "bias inside the instrument's class" is a statement of conformity with its decision rule named, as 7.8.6 requires and as ILAC-G8 defines it.

**The command**: `gea certificates --register C:\site\wells\<id>\records\certificates.jsonl --action conformance` prints every filed certificate against the items of ISO/IEC 17025:2017 7.8.2.1 and 7.8.4.1. Filing a certificate (`--action add`, the page's "File a certificate", or the API) now takes what 7.8.4 asks the paper to carry: `--uncertainty` with `--k` and `--probability` (7.8.4.1 a), `--conditions` (b), `--traceability` as the certificate words it (c), `--adjustment` (d), `--decision-rule` (e / 7.8.6), and `--calibrated`, `--method`, `--authorised-by` (7.8.2.1 i, f, o). Leave empty what the paper does not carry. `gea conformance` runs the self-test.

**What it writes**: per certificate, each item CARRIED (the register holds it), NOT CARRIED (the field is there and empty - the paper does not carry it, or nobody transcribed it) or NOT RECORDED (the entry was filed before the field existed), and COMPLETE when the identification, the laboratory, the serial, the dates, the result, the uncertainty with its coverage factor and the traceability statement are all carried. On the well page, the certificate table shows U (k) and the 7.8 standing with the missing clauses. In the drift report, section 9: for each certificate with a measured bias, a statement of conformity - the result it applies to (the tag's bias over the window, with its sample count), the specification (the certificate's accuracy class as an absolute band), the expanded uncertainty of the bias (k = 2, from the noise and the sample count), and the decision rule: ILAC-G8:09/2019 non-binary with a guard band equal to U - PASS below TL - U, CONDITIONAL PASS from TL - U to TL, CONDITIONAL FAIL from TL to TL + U, FAIL above TL + U. The probability of a false accept at the acceptance limit is at most 2.5 % when U is the 95 % expanded uncertainty.

**The number to check**: `COMPLETE` or `INCOMPLETE` with the missing clauses named on each certificate; `PASS`, `CONDITIONAL PASS`, `CONDITIONAL FAIL` or `FAIL` on each bias, and the acceptance limit printed beside the tolerance limit.

**What this page will not call a measurement**: a conformity decision that took the uncertainty into account when none was available - without U the only rule is simple acceptance (the acceptance limit is the tolerance limit, ILAC-G8's w = 0), and the statement says so and says the uncertainty was not taken into account. A coverage factor the certificate did not state - k is recorded as filed, and a k that was not filed is a gap, not 2. The content of the paper itself - the register holds what a person transcribed, this program does not read the PDF, and NOT CARRIED is the register's answer. A fault in the instrument from a CONDITIONAL outcome - a result inside the guard band is a result the uncertainty cannot place, and the standard's own word for it is conditional.
