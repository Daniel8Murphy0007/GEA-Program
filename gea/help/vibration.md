# Machine vibration

> The pump's record read the way an operations engineer expects: which ISO 20816-3 zone the machine is in, and - when it is not A or B - what the bearings are doing, by envelope analysis against the bearing's own defect frequencies.

**The command**: `gea workspace --path C:\site --action vibration-report --well <id> --record pump.csv --channel acc --vib-unit g --rpm 1780 --group 2 --support rigid --bearing 9,7.94,39.04` writes `reports/wells/<id>/vibration_report.{md,html,json}` and copies the record beside the well under `machine/` with its hash. The record is a delimited export whose first column is a timestamp or elapsed seconds (the historian reader) or a miniSEED/SAC record (the seismic reader; counts as recorded, so give the unit and `--sensitivity` units-per-count). The unit (`--vib-unit` in the workspace, `--unit` in `gea vibration`) is g, m/s2, mm/s or in/s. `--demod-band lo hi` declares the demodulation band instead of letting kurtosis choose it. `gea vibration --action assess --record ...` does the same without a workspace; `gea vibration` runs the self-test on a labelled pump record.

**What it writes**: the broadband r.m.s. vibration velocity in mm/s over 10 Hz to 1000 Hz (2 Hz to 1000 Hz below 600 r/min), the quantity ISO 20816-1 defines - from an acceleration channel by integration in the frequency domain inside the band, from a velocity channel by band-limiting; the ISO 20816-3 zone for the declared machine group (Group 1 above 300 kW; Group 2 from 15 kW to 300 kW) and support class (rigid when the machine-foundation system's lowest natural frequency is at least 1.25 times the main excitation frequency, otherwise flexible), with the boundaries printed (Group 1 rigid 2.3 / 4.5 / 7.1; Group 1 flexible 3.5 / 7.1 / 11.0; Group 2 rigid 1.4 / 2.8 / 4.5; Group 2 flexible 2.3 / 4.5 / 7.1 mm/s for A/B, B/C, C/D) and the margin to the next; the demodulation band (chosen as the candidate band with the highest kurtosis, because a bearing fault's impacts are impulsive and kurtosis is their measure, or declared); the envelope spectrum of the analytic signal in that band; the bearing's defect frequencies from its geometry and the shaft speed - BPFO, BPFI, BSF, FTF - each MATCHED (a peak at the fundamental within 2% or one resolution bin, at least four times the spectrum's median, harmonics at the same tolerance, and for the inner race the sidebands at the shaft speed) or NOT MATCHED.

**The number to check**: the zone, the r.m.s. value it was set by, and `FAULT FREQUENCY PRESENT` or `NO FAULT FREQUENCY MATCHED` with the matched frequency named. `PARTIAL BAND` on the zone means the record's rate cannot carry 1000 Hz and the value was taken to the record's own Nyquist frequency.

**What this page will not call a measurement**: a machine group or a support class - the record does not say what the machine is standing on, and both are declared. A zone from a record that cannot carry the band, which is marked PARTIAL BAND and not promoted; or from a record shorter than a second. A fault size - a matched frequency says the bearing rings at it, not how far the fault has gone. A defect frequency without the bearing's geometry - without it the envelope spectrum's peaks are listed and nothing is matched. A match on harmonics alone - the harmonics of one defect frequency fall near multiples of another, so the fundamental is required. An mm/s value from a record in counts without a declared sensitivity.
