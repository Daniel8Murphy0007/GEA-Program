# The charts

> A well's channels, stacked on one horizontal axis with a cursor that reads them all at the same moment, cut from every sample of the well's file - not from a thinned copy. Drag across a stretch to zoom into it, wheel to zoom where you point, double-click to see everything again; on a drilling recorder's export the axis switches between time and depth, the depth events are marked where they happened, the recording's sections are a click each, and the Wells page carries the drilling progress of every well on one chart.

**The command**: the Charts card on a well's page. Choose up to four channels (one per track); point at the chart for the values at that moment - each channel's mean in the bucket under the cursor, with its minimum and maximum and how many samples it holds, or the sample itself when the window is close enough to show every one. Drag across a stretch to zoom into it; the wheel zooms where you point; + and − zoom, ◀ and ▶ pan by half a window, Reset (or double-click) shows everything. The keyboard does the same once the chart has focus: ← → move the cursor (Shift for ten steps), + and − zoom, Page Up and Page Down pan, Home or Esc resets. Time or Depth switches the axis on a drilling recorder; the section buttons zoom to one stretch of recording. Table shows the window as rows. On the Wells page, Drilling progress plots each drilling-recorder well's hole depth against days since its first sample; point at it for every well's depth on that day.

**What it writes**: a chart cache per well under `cache\series\` in the workspace, written by the refresh from the same read that writes the reports - the index, each channel, which samples were flagged, and for a drilling recorder the drilling samples, the samples a depth table may use, the excluded samples and the running bottom of the hole. It is a copy: it carries the source file's size and time and is rebuilt when they no longer match, the first view of a well with no cache builds it, and backups leave it out.

**The number to check**: the sample count under the chart against the file's rows (`gea edr --file`); a bucket's minimum and maximum against the raw rows for a short stretch (zoom until the chart says every sample is shown); the depth axis's range against the gamma log's.

**What this page will not call a measurement**: a bucket's mean - it is arithmetic on the samples in the bucket, and the band around it is their range; a line across a gap - a stretch with no samples is drawn empty, never bridged; the depth axis as the hole - it is the drilling samples by bit depth, kept off hole a later sidetrack abandoned, the same samples the gamma log uses; the drilling progress as a plan - it is the running bottom the depth pass read from the recorder, stale and re-referenced depths left out.
