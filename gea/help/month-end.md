# The month-end packet

> A client's month was five or six commands and a walk through the reports folder. The packet is that month assembled once: the refresh for the period, then every report the program writes for every well, site, station and track, collected into one dated folder and one archive, with an index that says item by item what is there and what is not, and a manifest of every file's hash.

**The command**: `gea workspace --path C:\site --action month-end --period 2026-09` (or `--period previous`, the last closed month; `--no-refresh` collects the reports as they stand; `--catalog` hands the regulator's catalogue CSV to the SRA packet). On the dashboard: Reports -> Month-end packet -> the period and Assemble (a job), and for an administrator Schedule monthly (a schedule entry, `month-end-monthly`, on the day and hour given, for the previous month). The result is `reports/month_end/<period>/` with `INDEX.html` / `.md` / `.json` and `MANIFEST.json`, and `reports/month_end/month_end_<site>_<period>.zip`; each packet is logged in `records/month_end.jsonl` and in the audit (`month_end.assemble`).

**What it writes**: for the site - the dashboard index, the accuracy statement, the SLA month for that period, the FAT/SAT protocol, the model cards and the SBOM; for every well - the gauge drift report, the alarm event report, the well-test validation, the shut-in / build-up report, the data resilience report, the machine vibration report, and the certificate register's conformity against ISO/IEC 17025 7.8 as it stands; for every site - the SRA packet for the period (when a Seismicity Response Area is defined) and the site report; for every seismic station and track - their reports. Each item is INCLUDED with its files or NOT AVAILABLE with the reason (no machine record, no SRA defined, no certificate register, no well test detected, never refreshed); every step that failed is listed; a month that has not ended is marked OPEN.

**The number to check**: `N INCLUDED, M NOT AVAILABLE, 0 FAILED` on the index's front page, and `closed: yes` for a month that has ended.

**What this page will not call a measurement**: an item that is NOT AVAILABLE - the reason names what the site lacks, and the packet never stands in for it. A number in any of the reports - each carries its own method and declarations; the index collects and does not recompute. A month that has not ended - the packet says OPEN and the one assembled after the month closes supersedes it. A packet whose files no longer match its manifest - `MANIFEST.json` is the check, and a packet is rebuilt whole, never merged.
