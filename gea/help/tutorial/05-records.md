# 5. A well from its paper records
Who: office
Where: #/wells
Takes: 30 minutes
> A well known from the regulator's file, the completion report, an inclination survey and an old gas test - entered by clicking, each value kept with the document it came from.

## Steps
1. **Wells**, **Add a well from its records**: the well's name and, when you have it, the document - the PDF or the scan of the well file. It is kept verbatim and hashed as the well's source.
2. On the well's page the forms come one at a time, each with a **Source** box: name the document and the date it was filed. Fill **Who and where** first - the API number, the operator of record, the position - which puts the well on the Map and on the Operations & seismicity tab.
3. **The completion**: the depths, the producing interval, the formation, the nearest lease line.
4. **Casing**: a link to the Casing & kick tolerance tab, where each string goes in as for any well (lesson 13).
5. **Inclination survey**: the stations one per line as the form writes them - `1289, 1 1/4` for a measured depth and an inclination of one and a quarter degrees. The program checks the depths and the displacement against themselves.
6. **Gas well tests**: the shut-in pressure and its depth, the shut-in tubing pressure, the gas gravity, the temperatures, the open flow the document states, and the points one per line: `rate, flowing bottom-hole pressure`. The program fits the deliverability curve and says whether the document's open flow agrees.
7. Press **Fill the forms with the worked example** to see a public well file's survey and test in the forms before entering your own; then **Write the records review**.

## You know it worked when
The records review is under the well's reports, every figure with the document and date it came from, and the well is on the Map.

## A well that is not this site's
A well you are looking at for someone else belongs in a workspace of its own: Administration, **Workspaces**, **Make it and open it** - it opens in a window of its own with its own port.
