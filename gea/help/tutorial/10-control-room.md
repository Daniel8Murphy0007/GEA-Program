# 10. The control room wall and the shift handover
Who: office
Where: #/control
Takes: 15 minutes
> A wall screen read from across the room, and the handover note signed by one operator and acknowledged by the next.

## Steps
1. Open **Control room**, then **Open the wall**. One tile per well, worst first: its state in a shape and a word - Serious, Watch, Good, No data - with the reasons and the numbers that matter. It refreshes itself every 30 seconds.
2. Press **F** for full screen; **Esc** comes back. With more wells than fit, the wall turns the page every 15 seconds, the worst wells always on the first page.
3. The handover: on the Control room tab fill any of **Open**, **Watching** and **Done** and press **Sign and hand over** (the operator role). The note goes up on the wall until the next operator presses **I have read this** - the author cannot acknowledge their own note. Both are in the audit log.
4. A screen that should not sit under anyone's account: an administrator issues a **wall pass** on the same tab, named for the screen, for 1 to 365 days. On that screen open the program's address, choose **This is a wall screen** and type the code once. A pass shows the wall and the latest handover and nothing else; revoke it from the same tab.
5. The page asks the browser to keep the screen awake and says whether it was allowed; otherwise set the display never to sleep.

## You know it worked when
The doghouse TV shows the wall under a pass, and this morning's handover is on it with the night operator's name.
