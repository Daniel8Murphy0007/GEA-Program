# Simulation

> One well drilled again on the screen: the bit runs down the well's path in 3D among the mapped formations, on the clock it was drilled by, while a mud-log panel beside it grows with the hole - the formation, the rock and fluid volumes, the logs and the drilling parameters at each depth - and a readout says what the record carries at the bit. Volve 15/9-F-4 ships with its logs and Schlumberger's interpretation of them; every well with a drilling record runs on its recorder's own clock.

**The command**: the Simulation tab. Choose the well. **Play** runs the record (at 1× the whole of it in two minutes; 4×, 16×, 64× faster), the slider and the step buttons move through it, the space bar plays and pauses from the 3D view. The 3D view turns, pans and zooms as on the 3D tab; the path is coloured by its formation or by any track, the hole drilled so far is see-through with the string inside it, and the bit is the orange marker. The panel shows a window round the bit (choose its height, or the whole well); below the bottom of the hole nothing is drawn until it is drilled, unless you tick **show the log below the bit**. Point at the panel to read any depth; click it to take the bit to the moment it first reached that depth. Choose up to six tracks under **Tracks in the panel**. From a terminal: `gea simulation` lists the wells; `gea simulation --scene volve-f4 --at 3300` prints what the record says at 3,300 m; `--workspace C:\site --scene well:Well-30636033 --at 12000` the same for a drilling-recorder well in feet; `--json` for the whole scene.

**What it writes**: nothing. It reads the shipped Volve files (`reference/volve_f4_lwd.las.gz`, `reference/volve_f4_interpretation.las.gz`, with `volve_f4_logs.provenance.json`: the pinned public copy, the hashes, the licence and the attribution), the 3D tab's Volve excerpt, and for a workspace well its drilling record, its chart cache (built the first time its charts are opened) and its survey when one is attached.

**The number to check**: a value in the readout against the file it came from - the LAS curve named in the track's source, at that measured depth; the rock and fluid volumes summing to one (the interpretation's mineral volumes and its total porosity do, and its hydrocarbon volume is the porosity times one less the water saturation); the bit depth against the drilling recorder's own bit depth at that time.

**What this page will not call a measurement**: a bit moving between two survey stations - between samples close in time it is moved evenly, and across a gap in the record it waits; 15/9-F-4's bit as timed - its survey stations carry no drilling times, so it is stepped along measured depth and the page says so; oil or gas told apart - the interpretation gives a hydrocarbon volume and its parameters take it as oil of 10 cP; a density, porosity or fluid for a well that has only a drilling recorder - the recorder does not measure them; the plane of a path drawn from the recorder's MD-TVD pairs - the record gives no azimuth; the gamma index lithology as minerals - it is a shale index between two gamma picks.
