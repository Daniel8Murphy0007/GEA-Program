# GEA Driller - the directional driller's agent

> A program on the rig PC that reads the rig's own feed and the pre-drill model, places the bit in the layer with a position filter, and, every 30 ft, recommends in the driller's units what to do next - a policy trained by reinforcement learning in a physics twin of the lateral - with the driller's answer, every command, and the records pulled into GEA.

**The command**: on the rig PC, `GEA-Driller-0.78.1.tar.gz` from the release (or the kit's `installers` folder, fetched on the Audit / Update tab): unpack it and run `sudo sh install.sh` on Linux or `install.cmd` on Windows, once; edit its config - the EDR's WITS0 feed (`tcp`, the host and port; `serial`, the COM port), the pre-drill model (the target layer's top TVD at the heel, its thickness and dip, the gamma in, above and below, the deep-resistivity tool, the heel's MD and TVD), the tool's cap, the limits - and restart it. The driller's screen is at port 8790 of the rig PC: `http://192.168.1.60:8790/live`. It shows the section with the model's layer window, the hole drilled through it and - since 0.78.0 - the **position filter**'s band: where the layer is from the readings, as a geosteerer places it (a particle filter over the layer's offset from the model, its dip error and its thickness, moved every decision and kept to the guesses that explain the gamma and the deep-resistivity distance), with the probability the bit is in the layer, above or below it and the distance to the top and the base with its spread, sixty feet ahead; the agent reads the same estimate, and so does the textbook driller it is judged against. Then the recommendation in words - *Build +1.5°/100 ft (toolface up, 25% steering); 25 klb WOB, 120 rpm, 500 gpm* - with its reasons (what the deep-resistivity tool says, the gamma against the model's values, the model's bracket, the limits) and the confidence of each choice, the rig's numbers against the limits, and **Accept**, **Reject** or **Did something else…** with the operator's name. In GEA: the **Drill string** tab's card **GEA Driller** - the rig PC's address and the well, **Pull**: the records come in checked against their hashes, and the table says how many decisions, accepted, rejected, done otherwise, the hole under advice, the armed sections and the commands. **Open the driller's screen** opens the rig PC's screen from its address. Before there is a rig PC: **Start the twin as the rig** runs the driller's program on this computer with a scene drilling itself under the agent - **Open its screen** is the driller's screen with the twin behind it, labelled SIMULATION - and **Pull from it** brings its records in; **Stop it** closes them. Over a cellular modem the rig PC joins GEA-VPN as a member of kind `driller` and GEA pulls from `http://10.77.0.4:8790`. The closed loop: `closed_loop.allowed` in the rig PC's config puts the switch on its screen; an operator arms it by name for one section of at most 500 ft (**Arm for this section**), every decision then goes to the downlink as a command, and the loop disarms itself on a stale feed, a crossed limit, the section's end or **Disarm now** - and this version's only downlink is the null one, which keeps every command on record and sends none, because no vendor's downlink protocol ships with it. From a terminal: `gea workspace --path C:\site --action driller-pull --box http://192.168.1.60:8790 --well SWD-1`; on the rig PC `gea-driller sim --seed 7 --pace 3 --port 8790`, `gea-driller evaluate --scenes 20`, `gea-driller train --seconds 600 --out policy.json`, `gea-driller selftest`.

**What it writes**: on the rig PC, `decisions.jsonl` (every observation, recommendation, confidence, reasons and answer), `commands.jsonl` (every arming, command and disarming with its reason) and `surveys.csv`, each with a `.sha256` beside it, and `manifest.json`. In GEA, `wells\SWD-1\driller\` holds the same files as pulled, `manifest.json` and `last_pull.json` with the summary; the audit lines `driller.pull`, `driller.sim_start`, `driller.sim_stop`. The twin as the rig writes under `field\sim-driller\data`.

**The number to check**: on the rig PC's screen, the feed's age (a decision is made only on a live feed) and, in GEA's table, accepted against rejected and done otherwise - an agent whose advice is rejected more than taken is not helping, and the records say so. For the agent itself, `gea-driller evaluate` prints the policy against a textbook driller on twenty held-out twin scenes - the hole in the layer, the sweet spot, feet a day, dogleg, stuck pipe, losses - labelled SIMULATION; the self-test refuses a policy that does not beat the textbook.

**What this page will not call a measurement**: the twin's figures are a simulation's, however many scenes, and say nothing of a real formation; a recommendation is the agent's reading of the feed, the model and the filter's estimate, not a survey; the filter's band is a probability, not a log, and the hole's position on its screen is projected from the surveys and the continuous inclination, not measured at the bit; the hole-cleaning and balling the agent reasons from are the model's estimates; and "commands sent" is zero in this version by construction.

Related: `gea help drillstring`, `gea help recorder`, `gea help vpn`.
