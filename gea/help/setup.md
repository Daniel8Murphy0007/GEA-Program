# The setup checklist

> What this workspace still needs before its reports can be handed over, read from the workspace as it stands. An item is done because what it asks for is there - a declared surface location, a backup on the disk, a site record naming the client - and to do otherwise, with what is missing named. Required items are counted; recommended and optional ones are shown beside them. The Setup tab carries a badge with the required items still to do, and the Home page says which one is next.

**The command**: the page is Setup on the dashboard. Each item carries its own form or button: the surface location and its datum, the reference elevation (kelly bushing, ground level or derrick floor) with its source - or the ground level from the USGS 3DEP point service at the declared position, to check before declaring - and the US Well Number for each well; Create the site with its client and its wells; the backup folder, Back up now and the daily schedule; Refresh the reports. "Go to the next item" takes you to the first required item not done. At the command line, `gea checklist --workspace C:\site` prints the list and exits 0 when every required item is done, 2 when one is not; `--json` is the machine record. Where every value is known the item prints the command to run as it stands; where the operator must supply a value it names the values and where they come from instead. A drilling record's depth events are confirmed by a person against the daily drilling reports: on the page, or `gea checklist --workspace C:\site --confirm events:Well-30636033 --note "daily drilling reports, 30 Jun to 12 Jul"`.

**What it writes**: the checklist itself writes nothing - it is read fresh each time. Its actions write what they always write: a declaration into the well's record (`set-well`), a site record, the backup configuration, a backup, a schedule entry, the refreshed reports, each in the audit log. A confirmation is kept in `config\checklist.json` with the name, the time and the note, and in the audit log; when the drilling record changes - a different number of events or a different total depth - the confirmation no longer counts and the item says to check again. A US Well Number is declared as the well's identity; it no longer marks a producing well as a disposal well.

**The number to check**: the count of required items done against the count required; each well's declared position against the survey plat, its datum against the plat's, its reference elevation against the completion report, and its US Well Number against the permit - the checklist says a value is declared, not that it is right.

**What this page will not call a measurement**: a declaration - a position, an elevation or a well number is what the operator stated, recorded with who stated it; a 3DEP ground elevation as the kelly bushing - it is the ground at the declared position, NAVD88, and is offered only as ground level; a confirmation as a check the program made - it is a person's, with their note; a done item as a correct one.
