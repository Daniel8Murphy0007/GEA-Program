# Plan vs actual

> A well against its directional plan: at each survey station, how far it is from the plan - high or low, right or left - against the survey's own uncertainty in that direction; the bit projected below the last survey; the one arc that takes it back to the plan, with its dogleg severity and toolface. Each well's survey program names the tool model that weighs each run.

**The command**: the **Plan vs actual** tab: pick a well; the three tiles (off the plan at the last survey, the bit projected, the arc back); high/low and right/left along the well with the survey's 2-sigma band; the plan and section views; every station; the survey program, the bit offset and the look-ahead, and the plan file (the operator role); **Write the report**. From a terminal: `gea plan --workspace C:\site --well W` prints it; `--attach plan.csv --azimuth-reference true --md-unit ft --source "well plan rev 2"` attaches a plan (CSV, Excel or WITSML; `grid` needs `--grid UTM14N`, `magnetic` needs `--declination`); `--remove` takes it off; `--program "0-1500:MWD+SRGM, 1500-:MWD+IFR1" --source "survey program rev 1"` declares the survey program (metres); `--bit-offset 15 --lookahead 30` set the projection; `--models` lists the tool models; `--write` writes the report.

**What a well needs**: a surface position and a reference elevation, a directional survey, and a plan - the plan read by the same rules as a survey, its azimuth reference stated, never guessed. A well with a plan and no survey yet is checked along its plan on the Collision tab.

**How it is measured**: each survey station is set against the closest point of the plan in three dimensions, on the plan's arcs. The difference is split in the plan's own frame there: high (+) or low (-) in the vertical plane through the plan's direction, right (+) or left (-) across it. Each split is set against the survey's standard deviation in the same direction, from the well's survey program: off by more than 2 sigma is off by more than the survey's error model explains. The bit is projected the bit offset below the last survey (15 m by default), holding the last interval's build and turn rates. The arc back is the one circular arc from the projected bit, in its projected direction, to the plan point the look-ahead further on (30 m by default): its dogleg, its length, its severity in degrees per 30 m and its toolface (0 high side, 90 right, 180 low side, 270 left).

**The tool models**: the ISCWSA MWD Rev 4 model, and the ISCWSA / OWSG tool-code models that use only the ISCWSA MWD Rev 5 weighting functions: MWD and EMS with the standard, high- or low-resolution geomagnetic model (SRGM, HRGM, LRGM) or in-field referencing (IFR1, IFR2), with sag and multi-station corrections - 21 in all. MWD+SRGM is the ISCWSA MWD Rev 5.11 standard and the default; it reproduces all 144 rows of the ISCWSA's own Rev 5 example. A survey program weighs each station by the tool that surveyed it: an error of one run (random or systematic) stays with that run; an error of the well or of the field (declination, the field strength, the depth stretch) runs through every run. Gyro, axial-interference-corrected and inclination-only models are not here.

**What it writes**: the plan beside the well in `wells\<well>\plan\` (audit lines `well.plan`, `well.plan_removed`), the survey programs in `wellpath\survey_program.json` (audit line `survey_program.declare`), the audit line `report.plan_vs_actual`, and the report `reports\wells\<well>\plan_vs_actual.*` (PDF, HTML, markdown, JSON).

**The number to check**: one station's distance to the plan against the directional company's own plan-versus-actual sheet for the same survey; the projected bit against the directional driller's projection; the arc's severity against the motor's build rate.

**What this page will not call a measurement**: where the bit is - below the last survey the position is projected, not surveyed. The arc back - one arc to a point of the plan, not a re-plan that also matches the plan's direction there. A tool's accuracy beyond its model - each survey is weighed by the tool model its program names, and an undeclared program is taken as the ISCWSA standard MWD.

**Sources**: the ISCWSA error model (Williamson, SPE-67616-PA, 2000) at Rev 4 and Rev 5.11 (the surface tie-on, the course-length terms XCLA and XCLH, the enhanced sag and misalignment terms); the OWSG tool-code sets A and B, Rev 5.1, as carried in welleng (Apache-2.0), magnitudes unchanged; the ISCWSA Rev 5 example for test well 1.
