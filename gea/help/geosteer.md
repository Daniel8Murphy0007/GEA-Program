# Geosteering

> The office's geosteering panel. GEA Driller on the rig PC works out, every 30 ft, where the target layer is from the gamma and the pre-drill model, and recommends what to steer. This page draws one lateral as that program has it - the layer by the model and by the program's position estimate, the hole through it, the gamma read against what the model would have a tool read, where the last recommendation points, and what the driller answered - from the record pulled into the well, and, when asked, from the rig PC as it stands now. It is the directional driller's screen for the office: nothing on it steers.

**The command**: the **Geosteering** tab, once a record has been pulled from a rig PC into a well (the **Drill string** tab's GEA Driller card pulls the first one, or starts the twin as the rig on this computer). Pick the well. **Pull again** (the operator role) fetches the record again from where it was pulled before. **Follow the rig PC** asks the rig PC how it stands every ten seconds while the tab is open - the hole depth, the gamma, the up and down gamma where the string has the tool, how sure the estimate is that the bit is in the layer, the recommendation that is waiting and how many decisions have been made since the pull; an operator may also tick **pull each new decision into the well as it comes**, and the pictures then advance by themselves. Under the recommendation that is waiting, an operator may **Accept** it or **Turn it down**, with a note. **Write the report** keeps the panel. `gea geosteer --workspace C:\site --well "Pad 3 H-1"` prints the same, `--live` asks the rig PC once, `--pull` pulls first and `--write` writes the report; without a workspace it runs the self-test on a layer and a hole that are known.

**The pictures**: four, one measure each, all on the distance along the plan. **The section** is depth downward: the pre-drill model's layer in green; the layer as the position estimate had it at each decision in blue, its top drawn again with the estimate's own spread; the hole in black with a dot at every decision - green accepted, red turned down, grey not answered; and a dashed line to where the last recommendation points, the hole carried 90 ft on at the build and the turn it asks for. **The gamma** is what was read at the bit, the up and down gamma where the record carries them, the model's three values - the layer's, the rock's above, the rock's below - as dashed lines, and what those values would have a tool read at the place the estimate put the bit. **The probability** is the estimate's own: in the layer, above it, below it. **The plan view** is the hole right or left of the plan line inside its corridor.

**An answer from the office**: Accept and Turn it down post to the rig PC's own record under the name of who is signed in, marked `(office)` and noted as given through GEA, and write an audit line here. Only the recommendation the page showed can be answered - if the rig PC has moved on to another, the answer is not sent and the page says so - and only one not yet answered. While the closed loop is armed its commands are the answer, and nothing is posted. What was done instead of a recommendation is recorded on the rig PC's own screen, where the driller is. Arming and disarming are done on the rig PC and nowhere else.

**What it writes**: reading the page writes nothing. **Pull again**, and each pull made while following, writes the record under `wells\<well>\driller\` and an audit line `driller.pull`. An answer writes an audit line `geosteer.answer` here and the answer itself on the rig PC. **Write the report** writes `reports\geosteering\<well>\geosteering.json`, `geosteering.md` and `stations.csv` (a row a decision: where the bit was, what was read, where the estimate had the layer, what was recommended, who decided, what was answered), with the audit line `geosteer.report`.

**The number to check**: on the twin as the rig, the estimate's top against the true layer the twin's own screen shows; on a real well, the estimate's top and base at a few depths against the layer picked from the well's own log once it is run. `gea geosteer` with no workspace must end `OK`: on its made record the layer is 8 ft deeper than the model has it, the gamma is exact against the layer as it is and more than 10 API off against the model's, and the hole leaves through the top and is brought back.

**What this page will not call a measurement**: the layer's top and base - they are the position estimate's, worked out on the rig PC from the gamma and the pre-drill model, and a log run after the well is the measurement; "in the layer" - it counts the decisions at which the estimate put the bit there, and the estimate can be wrong; the gamma's misfit against the estimate - the estimate was fitted to that gamma, so a small misfit is what fitting does; the aim point - it is geometry, and the tool, the bit and the rock decide where the hole goes; the rig PC's figures now - they are as old as the page says. On the twin every figure is a simulation's and the page says so. No real lateral has been drawn here yet: the panel has been run on the twin and on a made record only.
