# The Seismicity Response Area

> What an operator inside a Railroad Commission Seismicity Response Area puts in front of the Commission, built from what the site holds.

**The command**: declare each disposal well once - `gea workspace --path C:\site --action set-well --well <id> --lat LAT --lon LON --datum NAD27 --api 42-329-12345 --uic 123456 --depth-tier deep --formation "completed below the base of the Wolfcamp" --channel-pressure P_surf_psi --channel-rate Q_bpm --rate-unit bbl/min --bhp-method probe --channel-bhp P_bh_psi`; define the area on the site once - `--action sra-define --site <id> --name "Gardendale SRA" --lat 31.95 --lon -102.25 --datum WGS84 --radius-km 9.08 --plan-date 2022-12-16 --triggering-event texnet2022yxab`; then every month - `--action sra-report --site <id> --catalog texnet_export.csv [--aftershocks id1 id2] [--start 2026-03-01 --end 2026-03-31]`. `gea sra` runs the self-test on a labelled scene.

**What it writes**: `reports/sites/<site>/sra_packet.html` (and `.md`, `.json`) - the area and the plan as declared, what is inside it with each well's depth tier, the daily record per well rolled up by month, the seismicity against the plan, the events the site's own stations located (when `--action associate` has been run on the site: each inside or outside the area, against the catalogue, no magnitude), the checkpoint schedule, and every gap by name; and `sra_daily_export.csv` - one line per well per day under the Notice's own parameter names (maximum surface injection pressure, average surface injection pressure, injection volume, maximum injection rate), the bottomhole pressure and its method, and the well's API and UIC numbers. Every declaration and every packet is in `records/audit.jsonl`.

**The number to check**: `Response:` in section "Seismicity against the plan" - TRIGGERED names the event, its magnitude and the deadline for the response group's meeting; NOT TRIGGERED names why. `Goal:` gives the days without a threshold event and the date the plan's goal falls on. The packet's front says COMPLETE or INCOMPLETE with the count of gaps.

**What this page will not call a measurement**: which earthquakes are aftershocks - the operator declares them with `--aftershocks` and they are shown as exempt, never decided here. A volume read off a pressure gauge - a well with no rate channel has no volume and no maximum rate, and the packet says NOT RECORDED. A depth tier from depth alone - the tier is stratigraphic, above or below the named formation base, and is declared. The TexNet reporting tool's own upload template - the export carries the Notice's parameter names and the mapping is the operator's step. A catalogue nobody handed in - the catalogue is a file the operator exports from TexNet, and its export date is on the packet.

The shape is the Commission's: the operator-led response plans on file (Gardendale, Northern Culberson-Reeves, Stanton) - a circle of about 9 km, a plan written against M 3.5, an 18-month goal, a response group meeting within 48 hours of a threshold event, quarterly checkpoints with Commission staff, and disposal wells in two tiers by the base of the Wolfcamp - and the December 2023 Notice to Operators on disposal-well monitoring and reporting in the Permian Basin, which names the four daily parameters and the three bottomhole-pressure methods. A downhole gauge this program monitors is the Notice's permanent probe.

Related: `gea help sites`, `gea help seismic`, `gea help data-in`.
