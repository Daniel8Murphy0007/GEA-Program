evaluation or internal use.

**Do I have to publish my site configuration or reports?** No. Only modified
MPL-covered source files distributed to others must stay MPL-2.0.

**Can I keep a fork private?** Yes, if you do not distribute it. If you
distribute modified MPL files, their source goes with them.
