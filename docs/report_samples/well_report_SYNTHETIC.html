7. What this report will not say

A declared number - the location, the elevation, the well number, a rate, an item, the AFE - is reported as declared, with where it came from, and is not measured here. A well path between survey stations follows the minimum-curvature arc. A mapped top is read as the two contours the location lies between, never as a number between them. A price is context, not a cause.
