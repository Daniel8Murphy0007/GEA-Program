1. Intended use

The instrument's published drift specification (psi/yr at its full scale), used to judge whether a measured residual trend is of the order of the aging the datasheet allows for (half the rate to twice the rate) or something else.

2. Out-of-scope uses

Not a measurement of any specific gauge's drift; not a warranty figure; not a substitute for recalibration records; no dependence on temperature or pressure is modelled - above the rating the rate is flagged, not changed.

7. Known limitations

(1) The rate is a specification, not a measurement. (2) Datasheet drift bounds are reference-condition limits; stressed-service drift can exceed them, and the program does not model that.
