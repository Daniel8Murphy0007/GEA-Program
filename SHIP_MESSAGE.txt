v0.34.0 — THE SITE MAP

Pad 3 has thirty-three structure layers in it, a stress map of forty
thousand records, three wells - and nowhere to see them together. The
layers answered questions about a position one at a time; nothing drew
them. This release adds the Map tab.

Everything in the workspace that has a position goes into one frame: the
wells at their declared surface positions, every structure layer the site
brought - faults and folds, formation contours, areas, points - the World
Stress Map's SHmax directions, the seismic stations and the response
areas, over the outline of the US states and counties. A well declared on
NAD27 is moved to WGS84 by its datum shift, about fifty metres here, and
the move is printed beside it. A well with no declared position is listed
as not placed, with the way to place it, and is never drawn anywhere.

Each view draws its layers at the detail it needs - a vertex that would
land in the same screen cell as the one before it is left out of the
drawing, never moved - so the half-million vertices of Pad 3's layers
pan and zoom without waiting. Click anywhere and every layer says what it
knows about that spot: the nearest fault and its distance, the contours
it lies between, the area it falls in, and how far it is from every
placed well.

The outline ships with the program - the Census Bureau's 2017
cartographic boundaries, with their provenance - so the map works with
no network at all.

Section CC is new, three checks. The gate is 389 checks. The standalone
guard is clean.
