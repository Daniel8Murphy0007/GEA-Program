v0.9.0 — THE SEISMICITY RESPONSE AREA PACKET

The first deliverable in this program shaped to a regulator rather than to
a client engineer. The Railroad Commission of Texas declares a Seismicity
Response Area around a cluster of felt earthquakes and expects the operators
inside it to run a response plan; the plans on file - Gardendale, Northern
Culberson-Reeves, Stanton - have one shape, and the Commission's December
2023 Notice to Operators on disposal-well monitoring in the Permian Basin
names the data. gea/sra.py builds to that shape and to nothing invented
here: a circle of about 9 km, a plan written against M 3.5, an 18-month
goal, a response group meeting within 48 hours of a threshold event,
quarterly checkpoints with Commission staff, disposal wells in two tiers by
the base of the Wolfcamp; the four daily parameters in the Notice's own
words - maximum and average surface injection pressure, injection volume,
maximum injection rate - and the three bottomhole-pressure methods, of which
the permanent downhole probe is what this program has monitored since its
first ship.

The operator declares what the program cannot measure - set-well: the
surface position and its datum, the API and UIC numbers, the depth tier by
the named formation, which channels are the pressure, the rate and the
downhole gauge, how the bottomhole pressure is known - and sra-define puts
the area on the site. sra-report then writes the packet: membership by
geodesic distance with every position carried to WGS84 first; the daily
record per well from its own channels, rolled up by month; the catalogue's
events against the plan with the trigger, its deadline and the goal clock;
the schedule; and every gap by name. The daily export carries the Notice's
parameter names with the API and UIC on every line.

What it refuses, and says so on the packet: a daily record from a stream
with no calendar; a volume from a well with no rate channel - NOT RECORDED,
never a blank; a depth tier from depth alone; an aftershock it decided for
itself - the operator declares them and they are shown as exempt; a
catalogue it fetched - the catalogue is a file with an export date. The
volume integrates the way a totalizer counts, each sample holding for its
own interval, because the trapezoid lost ten minutes at every midnight.

The gate is 308 checks (section BC is new). The standalone guard is clean.
