Added: the Slide sheet tab. Every slide and every rotated stretch of a
well, made from its drilling record and its survey: from, to, footage,
time, rate, weight on bit, the toolface held where the record carries
one, and what the hole built and turned across it. Survey course by
course: how much was slid, the dogleg, the build and the turn. And for
each run of an assembly: what the hole does per 100 ft while rotated,
and the dogleg per 100 ft slid.

On the Utah FORGE public pad, 16A(78)-32 has 38 slides; five of them
between 5,892 and 6,797 ft built its curve, and the dogleg per 100 ft
slid has a median of 7.5 degrees.
