v0.50.0 — PLAN VS ACTUAL AND THE SURVEY TOOLS

Each well can now carry its directional plan, and GEA reads the well
against it the way a directional driller does: at every survey station,
how far it is from the plan - high or low, right or left - and whether
that is more than the survey's own error can explain. Below the last
survey it projects the bit, and from there it gives the one arc back to
the plan: the dogleg severity and the toolface to set.

The survey tools are now named per well and per run. The default is the
ISCWSA MWD Rev 5.11 standard, which reproduces all 144 rows of the
ISCWSA's own Rev 5 worked example. Beside it are 21 OWSG tool-code
models on the same functions - MWD and electronic multishot with the
standard, high- or low-resolution geomagnetic model or in-field
referencing, with sag and multi-station corrections. A survey program
weighs each depth by the tool that surveyed it.

The collision check now includes wells not yet drilled, along their
plans, and a well being drilled along its survey and then its plan to
TD. The Collision tab draws the travelling cylinder: looking down one
well, every neighbour within a chosen radius, at its distance and
direction.

Gyro and axial-interference-corrected tool models are not in this
release.

The Plan vs actual tab, `gea plan`, a report with its PDF and the help
page are new; a signed dossier now carries the plans, the survey
programs and both checks. Section CS is new, five checks. The gate is
443 checks. The standalone guard is clean.
