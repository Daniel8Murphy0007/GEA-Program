v0.38.0 — THE CLIENT WELL REPORT

One printable document per well, and one for the pad, assembled from what
the workspace already holds. Nothing in it is computed anew: it gathers,
draws, and says where each number came from.

For each well: the well number, site, client, surface location and
reference elevation as declared, with their sources; the drilling record's
total depth, footage, days and hours on bottom; every depth event, what the
record did with it, and who checked it against the daily drilling reports;
depth against days; what each mapped layer says at the location; the well
path from its survey in plan and section, with the mapped tops as depth
bands; the declared cost with every rate and item, and the oil and gas
prices while the well was drilled. The pad report sets the wells side by
side on one chart and lists what each report still lacks.

A part the workspace cannot supply yet is not drawn as zero: the report
names it and says what would supply it. On Pad 3 today every well's report
asks for the same four things - a person's check of the depth events, the
surface location, the directional survey and the day rates.

The drawings are SVG made from the report's own numbers, inline in the HTML
and beside it as files, with captions and text for a reader who cannot see
them; printed, none is split across pages. The month-end packet now carries
the well and pad reports.

Section CG is new, three checks. The gate is 401 checks. The standalone
guard is clean.
