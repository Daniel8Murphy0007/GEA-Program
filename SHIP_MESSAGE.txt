v0.93.0 — THE PUBLIC PAD'S WELLS PLACED AND DRAWN

Added: the Utah FORGE wells are placed from their published directional
surveys, so the 3D, Simulation, Collision and Map tabs of the public site
draw the pad. Put the survey files in the folder with the recorder files
(the Public data tab lists them, under a megabyte each) and Make the site
declares each wellhead and elevation and attaches each survey; a site made
before is given them by making it again. Five published layouts are read
as they are - a text report, a planning package's report in a spreadsheet,
two reports printed to PDF, a spreadsheet's CSV - and the field's GPS
survey of its wells gives what a survey file lacks. Each path is checked
against the positions its own file carries (0.01 to 0.11 ft). 16B(78)-32
runs 297 ft from 16A(78)-32 at its toe.

Fixed: a depth entered wrongly at the rig and put right seconds later was
read as a re-referenced depth or a sidetrack. It is named a SPIKE now and
left out: 58-32's total depth is the 7,536 ft it was drilled to (it read
25,622.8 ft), and 16A(78)-32 no longer shows a borehole to 735,423 ft.

Said beside each well: the three vertical wells have only a ground
elevation published and are drawn up to some 30 ft too deep; two of them
do not say which north their azimuths are from.

Install from inside the program: Audit/Update, "Fetch the kits", "Install now".
