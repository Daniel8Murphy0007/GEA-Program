v0.83.0 — GEA-VPN: THE CLIENT PROGRAM

GEA-VPN is a standalone program now, for every computer that joins the
site's private network: GEA-VPN-Setup-<version>.exe on Windows (it brings
WireGuard with it and runs as a service of its own), GEA-VPN-<version>.tar.gz
on Linux. Installed once, it is ready for the connect information: the
office adds the member in GEA-Program, hands over its zip, and the person
drops the zip on the program's page (http://127.0.0.1:8795/) and presses
Connect. The tunnel is up from then on, at every start of the computer,
and the page says when the hub answered and whether the office answers
through it. Nothing is typed but a name.

Install from inside the program: Audit/Update, "Fetch the kits", "Install now".
