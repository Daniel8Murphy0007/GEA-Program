v0.59.0 — THE DECLARATIONS WORKBOOK CARRIES THE ENGINEERING

Everything the engineering tabs need now goes in through one workbook.

The declarations sheet - the one that already carried each well's
number, position, elevation, rates, runs and casing - now takes ten more
tables: the survey program and plans, how the pore pressure is read and
the pressures measured, the torque-and-drag string, the hydraulics
(string, hole, mud, viscometer, nozzles), frac stage tables and monitored
wells, production and the decline terms. Every row is checked against the
workspace before anything is written; one bad row and nothing is
written; the same workbook can be imported again and again.

It downloads as an Excel workbook, already filled with what is declared
(`gea declare --template pad3.xlsx`, or Download the sheet on the Setup
tab).

A well with no directional survey is now read on the drilling recorder's
own Interpolated TVD where a depth conversion is needed - Hydraulics &
ECD, and Torque & drag on an inclination-only path - labelled as the
recorder's interpolation, not a survey. An attached survey is always used
first.

Section DB is new, four checks. The gate is 478 checks on Python 3.13
and 3.14. The standalone guard is clean.
