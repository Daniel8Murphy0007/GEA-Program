v0.20.0 — THE SOAK: THE PATCHES THROUGH LINK OUTAGES, MEASURED

The load test said whether the machine carries the site's patches with the
page answering. The soak says what happens when the link goes down and
comes back, again and again, for hours. The site's patches, an ETP patch
and a SeedLink live station each connect through a relay on the loopback;
on the declared schedule every relay cuts every connection for the declared
seconds and then listens again, while the simulators keep producing. What
is measured is the supervisor's own reconnection and resume: per outage,
the seconds each source took to be back and receiving; per source, what the
protocol lost while the link was down - WITS0 has no replay, and that loss
is counted, never recovered; SeedLink resumes by sequence number, and its
gap count must be zero - and the page's answer time through it all. The
Soak Report says it with the outage table and what it does not call a
measurement. gea soak --hours 72 is the go-live run; Verification runs a
fifteen-minute one from the page.
