v0.92.0 — KICK AND LOSS DETECTION

`gea kick`: every drilling record with a pump output and an active pit
volume replayed for the mud that comes back. Each pumps-off is read as a
curve - the pit gain at 30 to 300 s - against the fingerprint of the eight
before it from a like pump rate, and one that runs above it is marked,
with whether the return line was still running. While circulating, the
ten-minute change in the pits is set against its own two hours, and a
change that leaves that trend is named by what the return flow did:
kick-like, losses, or mud added, moved or dumped at surface. A step is a
transfer. On a record still being written an event raises an alert once.
On the public Utah FORGE records: 1,864 pumps-offs, 1,429 read, 22 above
their fingerprint, two kick-like gains, three stretches of losses. The
Kick & loss tab; the assistant's `kick_detection` tool; `gea help kick`.
Section EK; gate 590.
