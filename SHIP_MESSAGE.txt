v0.35.0 — THE CONTROL ROOM

A monitor on the doghouse wall has to be read from across the room by
someone who is busy, and it has to say so when it is wrong. This release
adds the Control room tab and the wall it opens.

One tile per well, the worst first. Each says its state in a shape and a
word - Serious, Watch, Good, No data - so it reads on a washed-out screen
and without colour, then why in plain words, then the numbers that matter:
the alarms, the gauges, and for a drilling recorder the bottom, the bit,
the rate of penetration and the hours on bottom. The rule a tile is judged
by is written on the help page and nowhere bent: an unacknowledged P1 or P2
alarm, a gauge off with no explanation, a live feed down or long silent is
Serious; anything else active, explained or late is Watch. A recorded well
is described at the end of its record and is never called stale.

The wall refreshes itself every thirty seconds and shows how old its last
answer is. When the service stops answering, a notice goes across the
screen with the age of everything below it and every tile greys; a wall
whose sign-in has ended shows nothing old at all. F is full screen, the
screen is kept awake where the browser allows it, and when the wells do not
fit the pages turn, the worst first.

The shift handover sits beside it. The outgoing operator writes what is
open, what is being watched and what was done; the note is signed, keeps
the board as it stood, and stays on the wall until the incoming operator
acknowledges it. Both go in the audit log. A wall pass lets a screen show
the wall and nothing else, with no one's account signed in; the code is
shown once and can be revoked.

Section CD is new, three checks. The gate is 392 checks. The standalone
guard is clean.
