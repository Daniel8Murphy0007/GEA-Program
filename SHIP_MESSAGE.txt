v0.99.0 — THE TRIP SHEET'S ALERTS

Added: on a drilling record still being written, a trip that departs
raises an alert once, while it is under way or within half an hour of
its end - trip.fill for more mud at surface than the pipe accounts for
(a short fill pulling out, a hole that gave more running in), trip.loss
for losses. They go to the Alarms tab and the notification rules.

Added: trip.unread - a trip under way in which nothing in the record
moves with the pipe, where the trip before it was read. The hole is
being filled from another tank, or it is taking nothing; the record
cannot tell which, and the alert says so.

Changed: a trip marked not read is described as either - another tank,
or a hole that took nothing. It had been described as the first alone.

A trip is judged once the pipe has moved 1,000 ft. The Trip sheet tab
marks the record being written and the trip under way.
