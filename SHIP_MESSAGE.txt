v0.107.0 — PROCEDURES: EVERY CONNECTION AND TRIP AGAINST THE RULES DECLARED

Added: the Procedures tab reads, at every drilling connection of a
drilling record, the seconds the pumps ran off bottom before the slips,
the reaming passes, whether the pumps were off when the slips were set,
the seconds taken to bring them back up, and the weight-to-weight time;
and at every trip the pipe's speed, with the circulating and the flow
check before a trip out off bottom.

Added: rules declared with where each came from. Each connection or
trip is met, not met or not read; the count is by rule and by tour, and
the ones that did not meet a rule are listed with time and depth. With
no rule declared the page gives what the crews do now.

Added: on a record still being written, the alert procedure.departed.

Added: gea procedures, gea help procedures.

Note: a recorder's pump rate can go on reading for most of a minute
after the pumps stop. Where the record has the standpipe pressure, the
pumps are taken as stopped when the pressure falls.

It is the part of a procedure a recorder can see. Run over 580
connections and 547 trips of the public Utah FORGE records; no
operator's written procedure has yet been declared on it.
