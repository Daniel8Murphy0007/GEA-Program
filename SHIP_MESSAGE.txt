v0.33.0 — INTERACTIVE CHARTS

A well's page drew one channel at a time, as a fixed picture of a thinned
copy: fifteen hundred points out of a three-million-row drilling export,
one sample in two thousand. A connection, a jar firing, a stall lasts a
few seconds; at that thinning it was not on the chart at all.

The Charts card replaces it. Up to four channels stack on one axis, each
on its own scale, and one cursor reads them all at the same moment. Drag
across a stretch to zoom into it, wheel to zoom where you point,
double-click to see everything again; the keyboard does the same. Every
view is cut from every sample: each point on screen is a bucket carrying
the minimum, the maximum and the mean of the samples in it, so a
one-second spike survives a month-wide view, and a stretch with no
samples is drawn empty - a gap in the recording is never bridged. Close
enough in, the chart shows the samples themselves.

For a drilling recorder the axis switches between time and depth - the
drilling samples by bit depth, kept off the hole a sidetrack abandoned,
the same samples the gamma log uses. The depth events are marked where
they happened, the string coming off bottom is ticked when zoomed in, and
each section of the recording is one click. The Wells page now carries
the drilling progress of every drilling-recorder well: hole depth against
days, one colour per well, a cursor that reads them all on the same day.

The refresh writes each well's chart cache from the read it already
makes, so a three-million-row file answers a zoom in a fifth of a
second. The cache is a copy: it is rebuilt when its source changes and
left out of backups.

Section CB is new, three checks. The gate is 386 checks. The standalone
guard is clean.
