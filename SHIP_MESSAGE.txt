v0.53.0 — TORQUE AND DRAG

GEA now models what the drill string should weigh at the hook and take
to turn, at every bit depth, and sets it against what the rig measured.

The model is the industry's soft-string model (Johancsik et al.): the
string laid on the hole along the well's survey, element by element from
the bit up, with cased-hole and open-hole friction factors divided at
the deepest casing shoe. It gives the pick-up, slack-off and rotating
hook loads and the off-bottom torque, for the declared friction factors
and a fan of others - the broomstick.

From the drilling record it reads the rig's own hook loads while
tripping out and in and while rotating off bottom, and the torque, and
finds the friction factors that fit them, cased and open hole apart. In
the gate a record made by the model with known friction is read back to
within 0.02.

The drill string is declared per well, section by section from the bit
up. The worked example is Volve 15/9-F-10's survey with an example
string.

The Torque & drag tab, `gea torque-drag`, a report with its PDF and the
help page are new; the signed dossier carries the string and the model.
Section CV is new, four checks. The gate is 455 checks. The standalone
guard is clean.
