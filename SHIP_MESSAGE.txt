v0.48.0 — ONE RECORD, TIED TOGETHER

The last four releases each stood on their own. Now they read each other.

An invoice that differs from the daily reports and the drilling record is
an alert, sent through the notification rules, and cleared when a
corrected invoice agrees. The morning summary carries each well's invoice
check and yesterday's rig time - drilling hours, connections, the time
lost and what it cost at the contract's rate. A signed dossier carries
the contract, the invoices and daily reports as they were attached, the
rig-time targets and the casing, and both checks as they read the day it
was signed.

The declarations sheet now declares the contract, the rig-time targets,
block weights, casing shoes, and the earthquake watch and summary
settings - and the sheet, an invoice or a daily report may be an Excel
workbook, read without Excel.

With casing shoes declared, rig time is split by hole section: each
connection is held to its own section's best, each trip to cased or open
hole's.

Every route the dashboard calls was exercised against a read-only copy of
the site's workspace on its own machine, and every tab was driven in a
browser: nothing failed.

Section CQ is new, four checks. The gate is 434 checks. The standalone
guard is clean.
