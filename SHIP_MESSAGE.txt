v0.51.0 — FRAC HITS

GEA now reads what each fracturing stage did to the pressure in the
wells around it.

Attach a treatment well's stage table - stage, pumping start and end,
the perforations, the fluid and the proppant, from the frac company's
post-job report as CSV or Excel - and every well with a pressure gauge
in its record is read against every stage: the pressure's departure
from its own trend while the stage pumps and after it stops, the first
response and the fluid pumped by then, and what kind of response it was.

Poroelastic - the rock squeezed, falling as soon as the stage stops.
Mixed - a hydraulic connection, holding or rising after the stop. A frac
hit - the stage's fluid reaching the well, hundreds of psi or more, not
falling. The thresholds follow the published field classification and
are the operator's to declare.

With surveys, each stage's distance to the monitored well is given; with
an earthquake catalogue, the earthquakes during each stage are counted
beside it. A frac hit is an alert, sent through the notification rules.

The Frac hits tab, `gea frac-hits`, a report with its PDF and the help
page are new; the signed dossier carries the stage tables and the check.
Section CT is new, four checks. The gate is 447 checks. The standalone
guard is clean.
