v0.68.0 — GEA READER

A companion program for your clients, the rock inventory on a tab of its
own, and the operator window in the Start menu.

- GEA Reader: one HTML file your clients open in any browser, offline,
  to check a dossier you signed and read its reports. It makes the same
  checks as the checker inside every dossier, knows your site's key, and
  says plainly when a dossier is not valid or not yours. Reports tab ->
  Signed dossiers -> Download GEA Reader for your clients.
- Rocks tab: the 17 rocks, minerals and fluid the strata survey compares
  a log against, their published density and velocity ranges, a lookup
  for a measured density, and the checks against real cores.
- GEA-Program operator window in the Start menu and on the icon's menu.
  Fixed: it could not open on a computer without the operator-tier data,
  and its Start run stopped at the first chart.
- Fixed: the dossier checker now refuses a zip with two files under one
  name.

Install it over the old one: GEA-Program-Setup-0.68.0.exe from the
release. C:\site is kept as it is.

Acceptance section DN (4 checks) and E9.
