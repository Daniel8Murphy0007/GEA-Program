v0.47.0 — RIG TIME

What the rig did with every second, and the time it lost.

The drilling recorder already holds the answer to the question every
operator asks the morning after: where did the day go? The new Rig time
tab reads it from the hook load, the block, the bit, the pumps and the
rotary - drilling, in the slips, tripping, circulating, reaming, off the
hook - for every second of every well.

Every drilling connection is timed weight to weight, from the last second
drilling to the first second drilling again, with its three parts: off
bottom to the slips, in the slips, and back to drilling. Every trip is
timed by its speed while handling pipe, with the breaks inside it shown
beside it. And each is set against a target - the drilling program's, or
this pad's own best quarter, what its own crews have already done - so
the time lost is a number of hours, per well and per report day, and in
dollars at the contract's operating rate. The invoice check shows each
day's lost time beside the bill.

The rules were checked against the site's own drilling records, read on
the site's machine, before they were fixed.

Section CP is new, three checks. The gate is 430 checks. The standalone
guard is clean.
