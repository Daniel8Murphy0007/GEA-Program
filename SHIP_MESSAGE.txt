v0.45.0 — THE LATERAL, THE PRINTABLE REPORTS, THE DAILY SUMMARY AND THE KEY

Four smaller finishes.

The lateral. An earthquake near a well with a directional survey is now
measured from the nearest point of the well's path - its lateral - not
from the pad, with the measured depth of that point and the distance to
the earthquake's depth beside it. A protocol radius is read against the
well bore, which is what the regulators mean; a toe two miles from its pad
no longer hides an earthquake beside it. A well without a survey is still
measured from its pad, and says so.

The printable reports. Every client report is also written as a PDF beside
its HTML - the drawings redrawn, long tables continued across pages, page
numbers - and kept in report history with the rest.

The daily pad summary. Each morning, one page: what changed since the last
one, what is due, what is still missing. Kept in history, and sent by email
through the notification rules once a rule for pad.summary is set up.

The signing key. The dossier key can be retired and replaced: the old key
signs the hand-over to the new one, dossiers it signed before still check
out, and every later dossier carries the hand-over so a client who knows
the old fingerprint can follow it to the new.

Section CN is new, five checks. The gate is 424 checks. The standalone
guard is clean.
