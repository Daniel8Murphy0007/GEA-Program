v0.41.0 — THE DECLARATIONS SHEET

Everything an operator declares about a pad now comes in from one
spreadsheet, and a new tab, Completion, checks each ESP well's wellhead
penetrator against its maker's ratings.

The declarations sheet holds small tables one after another: each well's
API number, position and reference elevation, its directional survey, the
day rates, one-off costs and AFE, the fleet, the drill-string runs with
their components from the bit up, and the ESP penetrator and drive. Every
row is checked against the workspace and planned as new, a change, or
already declared before anything is written; one error and nothing is.
The sheet the program writes for a workspace is already filled with what
is declared, so a site fills in the blanks and imports it again. It is on
the Setup tab, and at the command line as gea declare.

The Completion tab keeps each ESP well's penetrator installation record -
the installer and their certification, the motor, the gauge distance, the
insulation readings, the void test - and checks the ESP drive, the motor
nameplate and the wellhead against the penetrator's ratings, one line
each: voltage, current, frequency, phases, hazardous area, gas group,
temperature class, ambient range and altitude. The Genco GPS-5 ratings
are those its maker's document prints; the GPS-5D and GPS-3 carry the
Division 2 of their titles until their datasheets are added. The tab
carries the permanent magnet motor safety note with its sources, and the
tool library gains the wellhead penetrator class.

The motor catalogue now holds 43 motors: two sheets added, each read
twice, and every other motor's figures read a third time and agreed.

Section CJ is new, three checks. The gate is 410 checks. The standalone
guard is clean.
