v0.11.0 — THE OSDU-SHAPED EXPORT

What a buyer's data team asks for on the first call: the site as the
Manifest an OSDU data platform loads. gea/osdu.py builds it to the published
well-known schemas - Manifest 1.0.0; each well as master-data Well and
Wellbore 1.0.0 with its API and UIC numbers as name aliases and the gauge
station as a vertical measurement; each well's record as a WellLog 1.1.0 in
the time domain, ZeroTime and the sampling interval and a curve per channel
with its unit, over a File.Generic dataset that is the historian file itself
with its size and SHA-256; each seismic station as a generic component with
its records as datasets; the site as the work product. The files are copied
beside the manifest.

Every position goes out twice, as the schema has it: AsIngestedCoordinates,
the point as the operator gave it with that datum's EPSG code, and
Wgs84Coordinates, with the operation between them written into
AppliedOperations - a NAD27 well says "shift 45.4 m". A point on an unknown
datum gets no WGS 84 coordinates at all and is named as a gap, because a
platform that indexed it would place the well tens of metres wrong. The
datum discipline of v0.8.0 reaches the platform intact.

What the operator declares and the export never fills in: the data
partition, the ACL groups, the legal tag. Without them the manifest is
written and marked NOT LOADABLE with the missing names. A structural
validation makes the platform loader's first checks here first. And what it
will not write: a seismic trace component under a schema not read in the
writing of this export - a kind not read here is not a kind written here.

And the console answers. gea serve opens the control panel in the browser
itself once its port is listening, and the window it was started in says
what happens as it happens - the page being opened, a sign-in, a job, a stop
or a restart. A server that answers in silence looks, from that window,
exactly like one that is still loading; the banner now says in words that
this window is the server and has nothing more to load. The launchers no
longer race the server with a browser of their own.

The gate is 320 checks (section BE is new; BB12-BB13 are new). The
standalone guard is clean.
