v0.61.0 — CASING DESIGN AND KICK TOLERANCE

Each casing string against the loads the hole can put on it, and the kick
each open hole can take.

- The Casing & kick tolerance tab (and `gea casing`): each string's burst,
  collapse and tension ratings by API TR 5C3 from its size, weight and
  grade - every collapse regime, the collapse reduced by the tension at
  each depth - matching API's tables (9-5/8 in 47 lb/ft N80: 6,870 psi
  burst, 4,750 psi collapse, 1,086 klbf).
- Against the textbook loads: burst from gas to the surface, held by the
  fracture pressure at the shoe; collapse with the string emptied; tension
  from the buoyed weight with the overpull. The smallest safety factor
  along each string and where, against the design factors.
- Kick tolerance below each shoe: MAASP, the tallest gas bubble the shoe
  can hold, and its volume at the bottom and at the shoe - in barrels.
- Read on the survey, or the drilling recorder's own TVD for a well with
  none, said so; the window from the Pore pressure tab or declared.
- Declared on the tab, from the command line, or in the declarations
  sheet's new [casing_design] and [casing_loads] tables; written as a
  report; carried in the signed dossier.
- An interval declared in measured depth is now converted to true
  vertical depth on the survey or the recorder's TVD.

Acceptance section DD (4 checks).
