v0.93.1 — THE RECORDER'S SELF-TEST ACROSS THE HOUR

Fixed: the field recorder's self-test failed when the hour turned during
it - it expected one file a channel, and the recorder rightly starts a
new file each hour. That stopped the build of the 0.93.0 kits, which ran
at 19:00 UTC; nothing was wrong with the recording. The self-test now
records across the top of an hour every time and checks the split: two
files a channel, the second starting on the hour, every sample read back.

This release carries everything in 0.93.0 - the Utah FORGE wells placed
from their published surveys and drawn on the 3D, Simulation, Collision
and Map tabs, and a depth entered wrongly at the rig read as a SPIKE -
with its kits.

Install from inside the program: Audit/Update, "Fetch the kits", "Install now".

Gate 593.
