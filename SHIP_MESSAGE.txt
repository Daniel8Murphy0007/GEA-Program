v0.5.0 — STANDARD PHYSICS ONLY, THE SECOND LEG BEGINS, THE REPORT SAMPLES, AND THE KIT MADE WHOLE

The program's own gauge aging model is gone. Until v0.4.0 the drift band had
a second edge: the datasheet rate scaled by a composition of constants
inherited from the predecessor program, with no field validation on record.
The model, its constants, the trims, the ratio bench test, the service-life
divergence curves, the case study and the desktop panels built on the
comparison are removed, not relabelled (two modules and one command deleted;
CHANGELOG names them). gea/gauge_aging.py returns
the instrument's published drift specification and nothing else, flagged
above the rating and never changed; the reconciler's band is half to twice
that rate; a tool with no published rate carries none; a datasheet JSON
cannot carry a model parameter. `gea bench` is rebuilt as the
datasheet-conformance test with a JSON-lines register - the bench record the
drift report cites as NONE ON RECORD until it exists. The guard blocks the
removed model's names, phrases and numbers in every tracked file; section AK
proves the absence from inside the package.

The second leg begins. gea/seismic.py reads miniSEED (Steim1, Steim2,
integer and float encodings) and SAC by content, writes both, fetches from
any FDSN service (TexNet, EarthScope), and gives Welch PSDs, spectrograms,
band power and the persistent lines above a running-median floor; the Steim
decoders are proven bit for bit against records written by libmseed, which
ship under gea/reference/ with their provenance. gea/seismic_detect.py is
the detectability test: one station's record against a CSV of known rigs,
a verdict per rig from band power in its exclusive windows against the quiet
baseline, the lines that belong to it, and the radius bracketed between the
farthest rig heard and the nearest missed; the report prints what it will
not call a measurement (a position or track - one station detects, it does
not locate). `gea seismic` with eight actions; the `seismic` help page.

docs/report_samples/: one rendered example of every client report from this
build (tools/render_report_samples.py; SAMPLES.md names each command; the
synthetic ones say so), shipped in the kit as report-samples/. The kit
carries every optional dependency, the desktop window included.

Fixed: the install-kit workflow's Windows job failed on v0.4.0 at `cd` of a
wildcard path under cmd.exe; resolved with `for /d`, and a manual run can
now attach the kits to an existing release (release_tag).

Acceptance sections AK (4), AL (5), AM (1); sections A, C, E, H, I, AA
re-derived against the datasheet-only engine. Gate 246/246 in the cloud and
on the development machine; standalone check 0 findings - numpy is still the
only dependency.
