Every tab of the control panel now has a lesson, and the gate holds the
course to it: no tab without a lesson that walks through it, no help
page without a lesson that names it.

site), the shelves (catalogue, rocks, strata survey, tools and ports),
an assistant that reads the site. The lessons on drilling and completion
end with what to look at on the practice pad and the figure to find.

long the rest take, finds a lesson by a word or a tab's name, shows the
office's or the pad's alone, and has a button for every page a lesson is
about. The Help tab lists its pages under headings in the order of the
work and searches the text of every page as you type; each page says
which tab it is for and which lessons teach it; the tester guide and the
IT security overview open there. On every tab, the help panel names the
lesson that teaches it. gea help --find toolface.

other by name, so the numbers stay right. Every tick is kept, by the
