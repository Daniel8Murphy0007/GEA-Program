v0.77.0 — GEA DRILLER: THE DIRECTIONAL DRILLER'S AGENT

A third program, for the rig PC. It reads the EDR's feed and the pre-drill
model and, every 30 ft, recommends in the driller's units what to do next -
build, drop or hold with toolface and steering ratio, the weight, the
speed, the flow, drill on or condition the hole - with its reasons and its
confidence. The driller accepts, rejects or records what was done instead;
everything is on record with its hash and GEA pulls it in.

The agent is a policy trained by reinforcement learning (PPO) in a physics
twin of the lateral, on thousands of scenes drawn fresh, and judged on
scenes it never saw against a textbook driller: about 70% of the hole in
the layer against the textbook's 41% (a simulation's figures, labelled so).

- Drill string tab, "GEA Driller": "Start the twin as the rig", "Open its
  screen" - the driller's screen with a lateral drilling itself; Accept,
  Reject, "Did something else"; arm the closed loop for a section and
  watch it disarm itself. "Pull from it" brings the records into a well.
- The closed loop ships as a design with a switch: this version's only
  downlink keeps every command and sends none.
- GEA-Driller-0.77.0.tar.gz on the release, with the trained policy inside.
