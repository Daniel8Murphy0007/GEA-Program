v0.40.0 — DRILL STRING

A new tab, Drill String, follows what was run in the hole and how hard it
was worked.

A run is declared from the bit up: each component a class from the tool
library with its maker, model, serial, outside diameter and length, and the
stretch of the drilling record it covers. The tab draws it as a build
diagram, and from the drilling record it measures each component's hours in
the hole, circulating, rotating and drilling, the footage, the gallons, and
for a motor its revolutions. A serialised component is followed across runs
and wells against the service interval declared for it, with its source:
OK, NEAR or DUE.

The motor tracker charts the differential on bottom against the maker's
maximum operating and maximum differential, the hours above the first, the
stalls, the motor's and the bit's speed, a torque estimate from the maker's
slope, and the rotor's turning and whirl frequencies. Its stability comes
from the frequencies a one-second record carries: block by block, the slow
oscillations of the surface torque and the differential (stick-slip lives
there), their distinct frequency and their strength, the spectrum at the
start of the run against the end, and a stated rule for what counts as a
change.

The tool catalogue now carries 41 downhole motors with the maker's
published figures, from MS Directional's Motor Handbook, each figure read
twice and compared. They are on the Tools tab, and a run can name one.

On Pad 3 all three wells show their whole records today, in under two
seconds each; the motor figures and the per-run view follow once the runs
are declared.

Section CI is new, three checks. The gate is 407 checks. The standalone
guard is clean.
