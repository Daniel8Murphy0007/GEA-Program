v0.46.0 — THE INVOICE CROSS-CHECK

The contractor's invoice, the rig's report and the recorder - three
accounts of the same days, side by side.

A daywork invoice bills days at rates: operating, standby, repair, moving.
The daily drilling report says what the rig did with each hour. The
drilling record says what the pumps, the rotary and the bit actually did.
The new Invoice check tab sets them against each other, report day by
report day, at the contract's terms as you declare them - the day rates
and the repair allowance - and says, for each day, whether the three
agree, and if not, what differs and how many dollars it puts in question.

It finds a day billed twice or for more than 24 hours; standby hours
billed at the operating rate; rig repair past the allowance at the
operating rate; a trip or a circulation the recorder never saw; a depth
or footage overstated; an amount that is not quantity times price; a day
billed with no report and nothing on the record. Each finding names the
file and row it comes from. The report prints as a PDF.

The files are read as CSV by their column headings, so your contractor's
own layout should read once it is saved from the spreadsheet; send one
report and one invoice and it will be fitted to them exactly.
