v0.10.0 — ASSOCIATION: THE STATIONS HEARD THE SAME THING, OR THEY DID NOT

The piece every monitoring pipeline is built around, and this program had
none of it. The leg could say what one station heard, what direction an
array heard it from, and where two arrays' bearings cross. It could not say
that the arrivals at three or more stations belong to one event, or where
and when that event was - so an earthquake inside a Seismicity Response Area
was known only from the catalogue somebody else published.

gea/seismic_assoc.py is three honest pieces. A pick: the onset of an
impulsive arrival by the short-term over long-term average, refined back to
the first energy, carrying its time and its signal-to-noise ratio and
nothing else. An association: one pick per station consistent with a single
origin under a declared velocity, found by a grid search over the ground
with the origin time solved at every node, accepted at three or more
stations inside a declared RMS tolerance, the worst pick dropped while more
than three remain, and NOT ASSOCIATED below three - never a located event
with a wide error. A location: the best node with the misfit region, the
nodes within one pick uncertainty of it, reported as an east-west and a
north-south extent and never called a confidence interval. An array is one
station, its sensors' picks reduced to one by the median.

What it refuses, on every result: a depth - declared, as every plan on file
works from a regional value, under a straight ray at one velocity on a flat
earth, named; a magnitude - it needs the instrument response and an
attenuation relation, and neither is in this band; an event from fewer than
three stations; a verdict on which position is right when the stations and
the catalogue differ - both are printed.

The scene caught two defects before any record did. The onset refinement
walked back into the noise and every pick came out 150 ms early - uniformly,
so the location absorbed it into the origin time and the RMS looked fine.
The fine grid was centred on the stations' centroid rather than the coarse
best node, so every event more than a kilometre from the centre was lost.
Both are in the gate now.

gea workspace --action associate runs it on a site and sets the events
against a catalogue export; the SRA packet carries the events the site's
own stations located beside the catalogue's. The gate is 314 checks
(section BD is new). The standalone guard is clean.
