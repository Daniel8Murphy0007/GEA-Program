v0.62.0 — KICK TOLERANCE ON A REAL GAS

The gas in the casing loads and the kick tolerance now weighs what it
weighs where it is.

- A real gas instead of a flat 0.1 psi/ft: its density at each depth's
  pressure and temperature, z by Dranchuk and Abou-Kassem (the
  Standing-Katz chart) on Sutton's pseudocritical properties for the gas
  gravity, the temperature from the surface and the geothermal gradient
  (0.65 gravity, 70 F, 1.5 F per 100 ft unless declared).
- Burst: the gas column is integrated up the well on that gas.
- Kick tolerance: the bubble's gradient at its own pressure and
  temperature, on bottom and at the shoe, and the volume carried between
  them by p V / z T.
- A swabbed kick - gas pulled in with no underbalance - beside each.
- A fixed gradient can still be declared (gea casing --gas 0.1); --gas
  none returns to the real gas. New: --gas-sg, --surface-temp,
  --geothermal, and the same columns in the declarations sheet.

Acceptance section DE (4 checks).
