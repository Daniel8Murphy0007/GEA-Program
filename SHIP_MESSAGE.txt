v0.27.0 — COMCAT: THE NATIONAL CATALOGUE READ AS THE PLAN READS TEXNET'S, PROVEN ON THE YOUNGSTOWN SEQUENCE

The seismicity response plan reads a TexNet catalogue export. The national
catalogue - USGS ComCat, and every FDSN-compliant service - answers the
same question for any site in the country in the FDSN text format, and the
program now reads that as it reads TexNet's: every event with its
contributor and its magnitude type, because a window across networks mixes
scales and the packet should say so.

It is proven on two verbatim public-domain extracts kept as fixtures with
their query URLs: the Delaware and Midland basins for September 2026, 28
events from TexNet and NEIC with the largest at M 3.4; and the 2011
Youngstown, Ohio sequence, nine events within 25 km ending in the M 4.0 of
New Year's Eve 2011, three kilometres from the Northstar 1 disposal well.
The gate runs the plan's arithmetic on that real sequence: inside a 10 km
area at an M 2.0 threshold, six events at or above it, the M 4.0 TRIGGERED
with its 48-hour respond-by, the goal clock started that night.

`gea comcat` prints the query for a position and a window, and reads the
file the service returns.

What it will not do: fetch the catalogue from a machine that cannot reach
the service; read one network's magnitude as another's; decide an
aftershock.

Section BU is new. The gate is 366 checks. The standalone guard is clean.
