v0.28.0 — TOOLS AND PORTS: THE TWO REGISTERS THAT HAD NO PAGE

Two registers have been in this program since the beginning and had nowhere
to be seen: the tool library and the port registry. The tool library is the
downhole and surface tools - each with the citation its numbers came from,
the temperature and pressure it is rated for, what it measures, the
telemetry interface it declares, and the aging rate its datasheet publishes.
The port registry is what brings those readings in: the file formats this
program reads and the eleven live protocols it speaks, each with what it
speaks to, what the site must supply before it runs, and what to rehearse
against when the device is not on line yet.

The new tab puts them side by side and draws the line between them. A tool
declares an interface; a port speaks one; and the tools whose interface no
port in this build speaks - the vibrating-wire readout, the thermocouple
card, the fibre interrogator, the piezoresistive family - are listed by name
rather than left out. A tool whose vendor published no numbers carries none,
says PARAMETERS_USER_SUPPLIED, and tells you to load your own datasheet.

The one piece of arithmetic on the page is the rating check: a tool string
hung in a well, each station's tool against the conditions at that station,
with the basis of those conditions printed beside the answer. A GEOQ 177
gauge at 16,000 ft sees 183.9 C against its 177 C rating, and the page says
so before the well does.

What it will not do: invent a vendor number; call a station within its
ratings a promise; use conditions it was not given; call a port a
connection.

Section BV is new. The gate is 368 checks. The standalone guard is clean.
