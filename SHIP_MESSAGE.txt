v0.15.0 — CONFORMITY, IN THE STANDARDS' WORDS

The drift report printed a certificate's accuracy beside the measured bias
and said "inside" or "OUTSIDE". That is a statement of conformity, and
ISO/IEC 17025:2017 7.8.6 says what one has to carry: the results it applies
to, the specification, and the decision rule - which takes the measurement
uncertainty into account. "Inside" with no uncertainty and no rule is simple
acceptance applied silently. gea/conformance.py names the rule and applies
it in the open: the tag's bias over the window with its sample count, the
certificate's accuracy class as the tolerance limit, the bias's expanded
uncertainty at k = 2 as the guard band, and ILAC-G8:09/2019's non-binary
rule - PASS, CONDITIONAL PASS, CONDITIONAL FAIL or FAIL - with the
acceptance limit printed. Without an uncertainty it is simple acceptance,
and the statement says so.

The certificate register now takes what 7.8.4 asks the paper to carry - the
expanded uncertainty with its coverage factor and probability, the
traceability statement as worded, the conditions, the calibration date, the
method, who authorised it, the adjustment results, the certificate's own
decision rule - and judges each certificate against the fourteen items of
7.8.2.1 and 7.8.4.1: CARRIED, NOT CARRIED, NOT RECORDED; COMPLETE or
INCOMPLETE with the missing clauses named. On the status rows, in the API,
on the well page, in the drift report.

What it will not do: take into account an uncertainty that was not
available; assume k = 2 for a certificate that did not state it; read the
PDF - the register holds what a person transcribed.

Section BI is new. The gate is 333 checks. The standalone guard is clean.
