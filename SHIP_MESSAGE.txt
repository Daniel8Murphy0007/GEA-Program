v0.25.0 — THE CATALOGUE ACCOUNTED FOR: WHAT READS EACH ENTRY, THE MEASURED FRICTION, THE CORE CHECK

The program ships 52 public datasets with provenance sidecars; a sidecar
says where an entry came from, not what reads it. Now the register does,
and the gate holds it to the code both ways: every entry a module names is
registered as used, every entry registered as used is named or is a log the
survey takes, and every entry reads through its own reader. Twenty-nine are
used; twenty-three are carried and say so, with the reason, and whether
they stay in the wheel is the owner's call.

Three uses are new. Every LAS log in the catalogue runs through the strata
survey with its provenance on the source line - six real excerpts from four
countries, one estimated and five refused with the reason, as the gate
checks. Seven laboratory datasets on natural fault and sediment samples are
read as what they measure and stated as the envelope a slip-tendency number
will be printed beside: friction coefficients from 0.21 to 0.90 on seventy
measurements, none from the Permian, against the 0.6 the law gives. And the
Volve core checks the published sandstone range the rock inventory carries:
bulk density per plug from grain density and porosity with the brine
stated, 65 of 70 plugs inside the range, on the model card as an evaluation
the inventory was not tuned to.

What it will not do: give the site a friction coefficient from other rocks;
invent a use for a carried entry; estimate strata from too little support;
call a derived core density a measurement.

Section BS is new. The gate is 362 checks. The standalone guard is clean.
