v0.94.0 — HARD LINES

Added: the Hard line tab. Declare a boundary - a lease line, a unit, the
edge of a project area, or an area to keep out of - as a polygon of a
layer or as corners typed in, with its setback and a warning distance,
and every well with a survey is measured against it in plan: how far from
the hard line it runs at its nearest and where along the hole, with the
survey's one-sigma beside it; how much of the hole is within the warning,
inside the setback or over the line; where the bit is, and how much more
hole on its direction reaches the hard line. The pad is drawn from above.
On a record still being written an alert is raised as the bit comes near.

On the Utah FORGE public pad the boundary is the project's own published
project area: 78B-32 stands 85 ft inside its south side, and 16B(78)-32's
bit would reach the east side in 582 ft more hole.

Also: an Open the site button on the Public data tab, so the public site
opens in its own window with one click.

Install from inside the program: Audit/Update, "Fetch the kits", "Install now".
