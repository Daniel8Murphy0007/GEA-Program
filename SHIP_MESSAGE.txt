v0.90.0 — A REAL PAD FROM PUBLIC DATA, AND THE BENCHMARK

`gea public-pad`: the Utah FORGE wells' published rig recorders - five
wells drilled between 2017 and 2023, Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 -
made into a site of their own, so the rig time, the roadmap, the drilling
alerts and the benchmark run on real records anyone may check. The
program does not contain the files and does not fetch them: the site
downloads them in a browser, and the program checks each against its
SHA-256, converts three layouts, attaches each well and ends every page
with the credit. `gea benchmark`: the wells ranked on feet per day, rate
on bottom, connections and trips, and each set against the pad's best,
stretch by stretch. On the five wells: 46,417 ft in 1,256 on-bottom
hours, 812 of them beyond the pad's best. The drilling record learned
four things from them: on bottom without a flag, a depth REAMED BACK, a
ten-second record's trips, a stand no bit drilled. The Public data and
Benchmark tabs; `gea help public-data`, `gea help benchmark`.
