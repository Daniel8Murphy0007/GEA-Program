v0.54.0 — DECLINE CURVES

GEA now forecasts what each well will make and what it is worth.

Attach a well's monthly production - oil, gas, water and producing days,
as CSV or Excel - and it is fitted by Arps's decline curves, turned
exponential at a terminal decline (the modified hyperbolic) so a steep
shale decline does not run to an unbounded recovery. The forecast runs
month by month to the economic limit, the first month that no longer
pays its operating cost, and gives the estimated ultimate recovery.

The value is the net cash flow at the declared net revenue interest,
severance tax and operating cost, discounted at the declared rate. The
price is the SEC's - the average of the first-of-month WTI and Henry Hub
prices of the 12 months before the as-of date, from the EIA series
shipped with the program - or a declared flat price. A forecast is not a
reserves report, and the page says so.

The Decline curves tab, `gea decline`, a report with its PDF and the
help page are new; the signed dossier carries the production and the
curves. Section CW is new, four checks. The gate is 459 checks. The
standalone guard is clean.
