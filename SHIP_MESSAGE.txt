v0.106.0 — WELLBORE STABILITY: THE THIRD SIDE OF THE MUD-WEIGHT WINDOW

Added: the Wellbore stability tab works out, at every station of the
open hole, the mud pressure that holds the wall up - from the three
stresses, the rock's strength and the way the hole points (Kirsch's
stresses at the wall, Mohr-Coulomb's rule) - with or without a breakout
the operator accepts. The window runs from the greater of that and the
pore pressure up to the least stress, above which mud is lost.

Added: the mud weight stands against the floor and the Hydraulics tab's
circulating density against the top, at each depth. A second picture
gives the collapse mud weight for a hole pointing every way.

Added: for a well with a drilling record, the rig at the end of the
record against the window; on a record still being written, two alerts,
stability.mud and stability.ecd.

Changed: the top bar has an eleventh heading, Well design, for the five
tabs that are models run on declared values.

Added: gea stability, gea help stability, a worked example.

None of it is a measurement: the stresses and the strength are declared
or estimated. Proved against its closed forms and on a made well; no
hole's caliper has yet been set against it.
