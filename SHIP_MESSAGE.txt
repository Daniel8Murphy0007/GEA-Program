v0.105.0 — THE DAILY DRILLING REPORT

Added: the Daily report tab writes the rig's morning sheet from the
drilling record, for one well and one report day: the hole depth at the
start and the end and the new hole made, the time log, the exact hours
by rig state, the readings while drilling, the mud the record carries,
the trips, and the alerts that began in the day.

Added: the time log is kept to the quarter hour, as a daily report is.
Each quarter hour is named by what the rig mostly did; a connection's
slips are counted with the work around them; hours with no record are
shown as that; the lines always come to 24 hours.

Added: an operator types the crew's remarks and the plan for the next
day; they are kept with a name. The sheet is written as one page that
prints, with its time log as a CSV, for one day or every day.

Added: gea daily-report, gea help daily-report.

The footage is the new hole the record shows being made; where the
day's depths say otherwise the sheet says so. Run over the 270 report
days of the Utah FORGE public pad. No sheet has yet been set beside a
contractor's signed report.
