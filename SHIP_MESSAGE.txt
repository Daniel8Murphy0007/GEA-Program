v0.108.0 — CHANNELS: WHAT A RECORD CARRIES, HOW GOOD IT IS, AND WHO READS IT

Added: the Channels tab, under Wells & data. For each channel of a
well's drilling record: the share of samples with a reading, the middle
value and its range, whether it reads one value throughout, readings
dropped, spikes, and where in the record it reads. Four checks of the
record as a whole: the clock, the bit against the hole, the hole depth
stepping back, and the pump rate after the pumps stop.

Added: every tab and alert rule that reads a drilling record, with the
channels it looks for, and the ones that cannot read this record, with
what is missing. The page to open when a tab cannot read a well.

Changed: the Procedures tab reads the pumps more carefully. v0.107.0
called pumps stopped when they had only been slowed; now the pumps are
off from where the standpipe pressure comes down to the level it holds
with them off. And the pumps have ten seconds at the slips: a pump
spinning down is not a pump running.

Added: gea channels, gea help channels.

Not a calibration: a channel that reads steadily can still be wrong by
a constant. Run over the five public Utah FORGE records.
