v0.71.0 — THE BLACK BOX, AND THE IMAGE

GEA Recorder: the field recorder for a drilling site, on a Raspberry Pi,
feeding GEA. GEA-Recorder-0.71.0.tar.gz is on the release.

- Records the ground sensors (geophones, electrodes) through a 24-bit ADC
  and the rig's WITS0 feed, every sample on one GPS clock, into hourly
  miniSEED files with a hash beside each and a manifest. One status page
  on the pad network. Nothing to type: power it and it records.
- Seismic tab, "Field recorder": the box's address, Pull. GEA fetches
  the files, checks every hash and makes the stations.
- "The image" on the same tab: the drilling itself as the seismic source,
  resolving the rock layer by layer from the ground stations - run end to
  end on a labelled scene, and the list of what the recorder must
  capture, which the recorder is built to.

Install GEA-Program-Setup-0.71.0.exe over the old one. C:\site is kept as
it is. The recorder's README (in its kit) has the parts list and the
install, one command on the Pi.

Acceptance sections DQ (4 checks) and DR (3 checks).
