v0.37.0 — ECONOMICS

A well's cost is the number every operator manages and no recorder writes
down. This release adds the Economics tab, and keeps the two kinds of
number in it apart.

What was measured: each well's days on its drilling record, the feet it
drilled, feet per day and hours on bottom, on the recorder's own clock.

What is declared: the operator's day rates over the dates they applied,
the one-off items, the AFE - each with where it came from, each in the
audit log. The cost is those rates over the record's time plus the items,
per well and per section, with cost per foot, per day and the difference
from the AFE. While the rates leave any of the time uncovered the cost is
shown and marked incomplete, and it is not divided by the footage: a
partial cost spread over the whole well would understate it.

Beside them, the market: the U.S. Energy Information Administration's daily
spot prices for WTI crude at Cushing and Henry Hub gas ship with the
program, public domain, and refresh on request; a rig count or any other
series comes in from a file with its source stated. Each series has its own
chart with the wells' drilling windows shaded, and each well shows what the
prices were while it was drilled. The page calls the prices context, not
cause, and computes no correlation across fewer than ten wells.

guard is clean.
