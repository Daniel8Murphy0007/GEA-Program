v0.49.0 — WELL COLLISION CHECKS

Every well on the pad is now scanned against every other, every ten
metres of each: how close they come, against how sure anyone can be of
where either one is.

Each well's position uncertainty comes from the industry's standard MWD
error model, ISCWSA Rev 4 - 27 error sources, propagated the way the
model says - and it reproduces all 112 rows of the ISCWSA's own worked
example. The Earth's magnetic field at each well, which weighs the
magnetometer errors and turns magnetic north into true north, comes from
the World Magnetic Model 2025.

The two are combined by the ISCWSA separation factor: the distance
between the wells, less the holes and a surface margin, over three and a
half times the uncertainty of that distance - with the errors both wells
share taken out, because a declination error moves every well on the pad
together. Each pair is levelled as the ISCWSA's rule does: reported
below 4.0, the approach limit below 1.5, revise the plan below 1.25,
below 1.0 only by the operator's dispensation. A company standard's k
and margins can be kept in its place, with where they came from.

The Volve template is the worked example: three of Equinor's released
wells, closest near the surface where the slots sit together.

The Collision tab, `gea collision`, a report with its PDF, and the help
page are new. Section CR is new, four checks. The gate is 438 checks.
The standalone guard is clean.
