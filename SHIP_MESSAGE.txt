v0.58.0 — HYDRAULICS AND ECD

GEA now works out the pressures the mud makes while it circulates, and
sets them against the window the rock allows.

From the mud weight and the six-speed viscometer readings, read the way
API RP 13D reads them, it gives the standpipe pressure part by part:
the surface lines, the string, the motor and MWD, the bit and the
annulus. It gives the bit's horsepower, jet velocity and impact, and the
equivalent circulating density at every depth, set against the pore
pressure and fracture gradient from the Pore pressure tab. For a trip it
gives the surge and swab, and the fastest a stand may be run or pulled
at each bit depth to stay inside that window.

From the drilling record it takes the standpipe pressure and pump rate
the rig measured while pumping, and sets them beside the model at the
same rate and depth. The difference is what the declarations leave out.

The Hydraulics & ECD tab, `gea hydraulics`, a report with its PDF and
the help page are new; Volve 15/9-F-10 is the worked example, and the
signed dossier carries the declarations and the results.

Section DA is new, four checks. The gate is 474 checks, run on Python
3.13 and 3.14. The standalone guard is clean.
