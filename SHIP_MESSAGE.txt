v0.42.0 — OPERATIONS AND SEISMICITY

A new tab, Operations & Seismicity, sets each well's drilling, completion
and disposal against the earthquake catalogue around it.

Every event within 20 km of a well appears on a time line by its distance
from the well, sized by magnitude, and is named as during an operation,
in the months after one, or outside. When a drilling record covers the
moment, the page says what the rig was doing and how deep the hole was.
Each event is read against a regulator's protocol - British Columbia,
Alberta, Oklahoma, Ohio or Texas, each with its document - for the level
it would reach and the action that level requires. The rate of
earthquakes near the well while it works is compared with the rest of
the record by an exact test, and the page says plainly that a
coincidence in time and place is not a cause.

Catalogue exports are attached with the window and circle they were
queried for; the tab draws the ComCat query around the wells for the site
to run, since the program does not reach the service itself. Operation
windows are declared with their source, here or in the declarations
sheet, which gains two tables.

The Mahoning Valley in Ohio ships as a worked example from the public
record: the 2011 Youngstown earthquakes against the Northstar 1 injection
year, and the 2014 Poland Township earthquakes against a stimulation,
with the USGS catalogue from 1990 to 2026.

Section CK is new, three checks. The gate is 413 checks. The standalone
guard is clean.
