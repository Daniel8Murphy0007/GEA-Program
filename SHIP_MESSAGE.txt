v0.44.0 — THE EARTHQUAKE WATCH AND THE ALERTS

The program now watches while a well works, and says so when something
needs a person.

The live earthquake watch. While an operation is under way at a well with
a position, the program reads the USGS earthquake catalogue from the
site's machine every few minutes - the real-time feed USGS asks automated
programs to use, and the catalogue service for any gap. An earthquake
near the well during or after the operation is an alert; one that reaches
the chosen regulator's protocol level is an alert naming the level and
its action, and a later revision of its magnitude to a higher level is a
new one. A watch that cannot reach the catalogue for three intervals says
so - a silent watch is not a quiet one. The watch card on the Operations
& Seismicity tab turns it on or off, sets the interval, and polls on
demand.

The alerts. The same machinery covers what nobody watches by hand: a
drill-string component whose declared service interval is used up, an
ESP penetrator check outside its rating, and a signed dossier the
workspace no longer matches - each raised once when it begins and once
when it clears. Every alert is listed on the Alarms tab, and sent by email
or webhook through the notification rules the site sets up.

Section CM is new, three checks. The gate is 419 checks. The standalone
guard is clean.
