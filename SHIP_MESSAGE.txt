v0.97.0 — THE TOP BAR UNDER HEADINGS

Changed: the 53 tabs sit under ten headings in the order of the work -
Start; Wells & data; Watch & report; Well paths; Drilling; Completion &
production; Disposal; Seismic; Keep it running; Learn. The bar is two
rows where it had been five to seven. A heading opens its tabs without
leaving the page, the heading of the tab you are on is marked, and a
heading carries the count of what needs attention under it. The search
box finds a tab by its name.

Added: a boundary declared on the Hard line tab is drawn on the 3D tab,
its line carried straight down to the deepest hole.

Changed: the Map opens on the placed wells and the boundary about them
with two kilometres of ground around, not thirteen.

Fixed: two workspaces that had used the same port were both shown
running on it, and opening the one that was not could open the other's
control panel under its name. Each panel now says which workspace it is
(a hash of its folder), the Workspaces table asks, and a workspace whose
port another holds starts on a free one.
