v0.81.0 — GEA DRILLER: THE VIBRATION INDEX FROM THE FEED

A rig without a downhole vibration tool left the agent blind to the
stick-slip and the whirl it was trained to ease. The rig PC now reads a
vibration index off the surface channels every EDR sends - the torque's,
the weight's and the hook load's oscillation over the last minute, scaled
onto the index the agent was trained with - and the downhole tool's item
wins whenever it comes. The screen's tile and every decision say which it
read; the closed loop's limit trips on either. The twin as the rig emits
the same surface channels, and shadow mode reads the index off the
export's seconds, foot by foot, and says how often the record stood over
the limit and how often the agent would have eased there. A surface
proxy, labelled.

Install from inside the program: Audit/Update, "Fetch the kits", "Install now".
