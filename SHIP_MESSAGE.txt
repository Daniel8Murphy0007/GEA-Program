v0.6.0 — THE SECOND LEG WHOLE: THE READER PROVEN ON REAL FILES, THE RESPONSE, THE ARRAY STEP, AND THE SEISMIC PAGE

The reader proven on real files. On a corpus of about ninety miniSEED files
from real stations and odd recorders (the obspy test data, fetched with pip,
never redistributed), with libmseed as the reference: 76 files sample-exact,
5 text files skipped by design, no mismatches, and SAC 12 of 12 against
obspy. What that found: the Steim byte-order rules of little-endian data
(8-bit differences in memory order), the meaning of the byte-order flag,
the data-offset-zero case, text records, and the gain-ranged formats of the
old networks - all read from the reference's own source. A record that
cannot be read is written down and stepped over. tools/seismic_reader_check.py
repeats the check anywhere.

Physical units. gea/seismic_response.py reads FDSN StationXML into its
stages (poles and zeros, gains, FIR and IIR coefficients with their delay
corrections), evaluates the response as evalresp does - proven on IRIS
IU.ANMO.10.BHZ to 1e-5 in amplitude and 1e-6 degree in phase - checks the
stages against the declared sensitivity, and removes the response with a
water level and pre-filter to m/s, m or m/s^2; a known motion through the
real response and back comes out with no error in the band. A file whose
stages disagree with their own sensitivity, a polynomial stage, a channel
the file does not cover: named and not applied; the record stays in counts,
labelled. The IRIS StationXML and evalresp's numbers ship as reference data.

The array step. gea/seismic_array.py: Bartlett and Capon beamforming over a
slowness grid, refined around the maximum; the array response function's
half-power width beside every bearing and its aliasing lobes named;
coherence judged per frequency bin at the found slowness; the weighted
crossing of arrays' bearings with its ellipse; lag location with the
velocity named as an input; the array detectability test. On the labelled
scene a 1.2 km nine-sensor array points at rigs from 6 to 45 km within 0.3
degrees against a 7-degree tolerance. Nothing is claimed from one array but
a direction.

The Seismic page. A station or an array is a thing in the workspace beside
the wells: added by upload or `gea workspace --action add-seismic` with its
record(s), position, band, rigs list and station file, copied in verbatim
and hashed; refreshed as a job that runs the whole leg and writes the new
Seismic Station Report and the machine JSONs under reports/seismic/; read
on its own page with the spectrum drawn, the lines, the detectability
verdicts and the array bearing, the limits printed under each card. Home
counts the stations and the listed rigs heard. The sample report joins
docs/report_samples/ and the kit.

Acceptance sections AN (4), AO (4), AP (4); AL and AM extended. Gate
258/258 in the cloud and on the development machine; standalone check 0
findings - numpy is still the only dependency.
