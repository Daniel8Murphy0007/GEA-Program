v0.39.0 — SIMULATION

A new tab, Simulation, drills a well again on the screen.

The bit runs down the well's path in 3D among the mapped formations, with
the drill string inside the hole drilled so far, on the clock the well was
drilled by. Beside it a mud-log panel grows with the hole: the formation,
the rock and fluid volumes, the logs and the drilling parameters at each
depth. A readout says what the record carries at the bit, or at any depth
under the pointer, and a click on the panel takes the bit to the moment it
first reached that depth.

Volve 15/9-F-4 ships with the logs of its reservoir section and
Schlumberger's interpretation of them: the minerals and the porosity, the
water and the hydrocarbon in the pores, the permeability. Its survey was
run in one go and carries no drilling times, so its bit is stepped along
measured depth and the page says so. Volve F-5 and F-10 play on their
survey stations' own times. Every well with a drilling record plays on its
recorder's own clock, with its channels by depth; on Pad 3 all three wells
do, each built in under three seconds.

Nothing here is modelled: every value drawn is one the well's own record
carries. A drilling recorder does not measure density, porosity or the
fluids, and the page names what it does not show. A path drawn from the
recorder's MD-TVD pairs lies in one vertical plane whose direction the
record does not give, until the survey is attached.

Section CH is new, three checks. The gate is 404 checks. The standalone
guard is clean.
