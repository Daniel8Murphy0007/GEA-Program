v0.64.0 — ONE CASING PROGRAM

A well's casing is now declared in one place.

- The casing program (the Casing & kick tolerance tab, gea casing, or the
  declarations sheet's [casing] table) is read by rig time, hydraulics,
  torque and drag and the casing design alike - no more declaring the
  shoes, the hole and the strings three times.
- A string needs only its size and shoe; its weight gives the bore; with
  its weight, grade, hole and mud it is checked for burst, collapse and
  tension, and until then the tab says what is missing.
- A shoe declared on the Rig time tab goes into the program, keeping what
  the program holds about that string.
- Hydraulics reads the hole from the program: each depth's innermost
  bore, a liner from its top, then the bit.
- Nothing already declared is lost: earlier rig-time shoes are carried
  in; a shoe or a hydraulics hole that disagrees is listed on the Casing
  tab. The workbook's old [casing] layout and [casing_design] still read.
