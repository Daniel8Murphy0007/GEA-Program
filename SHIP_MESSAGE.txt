v0.60.0 — EVERY TAB AND BUTTON ANSWERS

The control panel was driven end to end in a browser - every tab, every
well page, every button that does not change or remove something, every
Scene and Well choice - and the faults it found are fixed.

- The Verification tab opens (it used a value only the Seismic tab set up).
- The Rig time tab opens with a live well whose stream has not arrived yet.
- Add a catalogue well takes only an entry that carries a downhole gauge
  series, a well inside it and the gauge depth; the form lists only those.
- One well that cannot be read never stops Refresh every report or the
  month-end again: it is written down with why, shown NOT REPORTED on the
  overview and the printed dashboard, and named by the refresh job and the
  month-end packet, while every other well is reported.
- A live seismic station with no recording yet is listed as such in the
  month-end instead of stopping it.
- An empty field is answered in the form's words ("a number is needed: a
  field was left empty"), not Python's; the configuration buttons and the
  folder watch say what to choose first; a well with nothing to chart says
  so without an error in the browser's console.

Acceptance section DC (4 checks).
