v0.70.0 — A WELL FROM ITS RECORDS, BY CLICKING

Load a well from its paper records without typing a command.

- Wells tab, "Add a well from its records": name the well and attach the
  well file (PDF). Then fill its page form by form - who and where, the
  completion, the inclination survey, the gas well tests - each with the
  document it came from. "Fill the forms with the worked example" shows
  how with a public Texas well file.
- The page checks the papers against themselves: the survey's depth and
  displacement, the gas test's curve, its rates and its shut-in pressure.
  "Write the records review" writes the report; the well's signed dossier
  carries it with the document.
- Administration, "Workspaces": make a separate workspace for a demo or a
  client's well and open it in a window of its own, so it never mixes
  with Pad 3.
- The program starts correctly whatever folder it is started from.

Install GEA-Program-Setup-0.70.0.exe over the old one. C:\site is kept
as it is. GEA-Reader-Setup-0.70.0.exe is on the release too.

Acceptance section DP (6 checks).
