v0.7.0 — THE TIME DIMENSION: TRACKS FROM TWO ARRAYS, THE SAR PANEL, THE AUDIT / UPDATE TAB, THE PERMITS IMPORTER, AND THE KIT MADE THE COMMIT

The time dimension. gea/seismic_track.py runs the array step window after
window. A bearing history per array: the beam on every window, a bearing
with the array's own tolerance where it was coherent, a gap where it was
not, change points where the bearing moved beyond the tolerance against
the running mean. A position history from two or more arrays crossed
window by window: a position only where two arrays were coherent, the
bearings crossed at 15 degrees or more and the point lies in front of every
array; every other window a gap with its reason; every position with its
1-sigma ellipse. The track: the principal line through the longest
continuous segment, headed in the direction of time, with heading, length
and rate - and positions that jump farther than their ellipses allow are
segments listed apart, never joined (a coherent background crossing in a
quiet hour is not the rig an hour later; the first run showed exactly that
and the rule came from it). The verdict against ground truth - the
lateral's surveyed points, or a permit's surface hole with its date,
interpolated to each position's time - TRACKED, PARTIAL, NOT_TRACKED or
INSUFFICIENT, with the hit fraction and the heading difference. The
labelled scene is a bit advancing 3 km along a lateral on heading 80 past
two arrays: TRACKED, heading within a degree, every position inside its
ellipse. `gea seismic --action bearings | track | track-selftest`.

Tracks on the dashboard. Two or more array stations and an optional truth
CSV make a track in the workspace; its refresh writes every array's
bearing history, the positions, the verdict and the new Seismic Track
Report under reports/seismic/tracks/; the Tracks card on the Seismic page,
the track view with the map (every position's ellipse drawn from its
covariance, a slider through time), the bearings over time and the
positions with their hits; a home tile; `gea workspace --action add-track |
refresh-track | remove-track`; five routes.

The SAR panel. On the Seismic page: a control panel and a screen that plays
the leg's arithmetic forward in time on a labelled synthetic scene - the
trace, the spectrogram column arriving, the beam power map swinging onto
the working rig, the map with the bearing and the tolerance wedge, the
tally per rig, and at the close the array detectability test's verdict;
the second scene is the lateral, with both arrays' bearings, the crossing
and the track drawn as it is earned and the tracker's verdict at the end.
Every frame is stamped SIMULATION_SELF_TEST and says "not a measurement of
any ground"; a real record never goes through the film.

The Audit / Update tab. The whole audit log with filters and a CSV; the
program against PyPI with `gea update` as a job (pip --upgrade from the
same Python, then restart - the program never restarts itself; an offline
kit is updated by installing the newer kit); every report against its
source with `workspace --action refresh-all` as a job.

The permits importer. `gea permits` turns a permit export into the rigs
CSV the tests read: columns found by name with a mapping file to override,
the approval date used only where the spud date is empty and said so in
the row, end dates assumed and counted, rows without a position or a date,
duplicates and rigs outside a radius dropped and counted, all in an import
note. `add-seismic --permits` and the page's add form take the export.

The kit made the commit. Three v0.6.0 kit runs installed and gated PyPI's
wheel of the same version, because pip took the index's copy over the one
just built from the checkout. The builder now downloads only the built
wheel's own requirements, asserts the package wheel it ships is the one it
built, and records the commit; the main-push path creates the release when
the tag exists. Also: the installed kit's gate no longer asks for a
checkout-only file; a missing input file at the prompt is one line, not a
traceback; the tester guide carries no gate count to go stale; doctor finds
a per-user launcher.

Gate 266 (sections AQ, AR, AS new), green from the checkout, from the
installed wheel and on the development machine; 19 report samples
re-rendered at this build; sixteen help pages. Nothing in this release is a
measurement of any ground: the tracker's numbers are proven on a labelled
scene, and the first real record is still to come.
