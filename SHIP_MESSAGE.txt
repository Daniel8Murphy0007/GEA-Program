v0.16.0 — WITSML 2.x OVER ETP: THE STORE PUSHES

The newer rigs and stores do not poll. Energistics ETP v1.2 is a WebSocket
session, Avro-encoded to the published schemas, on which the customer names
the channels it wants and the store pushes every value as it is written.
gea/etp.py is that customer: the handshake with the subprotocol and the
binary encoding, RequestSession for Discovery, Store and ChannelSubscribe,
the channel metadata for the URIs the site's mapping declares (a URI the
store does not have is named as missing), the subscription, and each
ChannelData item as a record under the mapping's tag and unit at the store's
own index - microseconds since the epoch - with the arrival time beside it,
so the latency is measured per record. The codec is driven by the
specification's own schemas, carried in the package; client message ids
even, the store's odd; a ProtocolException carried with its code; a client
or a store without the subprotocol refused at the handshake.

It runs where every other port runs: the patch supervisor (CONNECTED with
the tags, units and latency; records on disk; reconnect with backoff), the
service and its config validation, the page's patch form, `gea etp` with
record and replay, `gea etp-sim` as a two-channel store to rehearse against,
`gea etp --selftest` on the loopback.

The doctor now names a panel that was started through the gea.exe launcher:
pip cannot replace that launcher while the panel runs, so the program
cannot be updated in place - stop, update, start with `python -m gea serve`.

What it will not do: give a time to a depth-indexed sample; name a tag it
was not told; convert a unit it did not read; decode a message it does not
know; talk to a store that is not ETP.

Section BJ is new. The gate is 337 checks. The standalone guard is clean.
