v0.63.0 — TRUSTED TIMESTAMPS ON THE SIGNED DOSSIER

A dossier's time no longer rests on the signing machine's clock alone.

- Trusted timestamps (RFC 3161): a public time-stamping authority signs,
  at its own time, the SHA-256 of a dossier's signature, and the token
  rides in the dossier. Only that hash and a random number leave the
  machine. Off until the site names a service (DigiCert, Sectigo, or its
  own) on the Reports tab or with gea dossier --timestamp-url.
- The check - in the program and in every dossier's standalone checker -
  verifies the authority's signature (RSA or ECDSA), its certificate, and
  its chain to a root the reader's computer already trusts, and prints
  the authority's time beside the machine's.
- A service that cannot be reached leaves the dossier signed, marked not
  stamped with why; Stamp adds the token later.
- Checked against tokens OpenSSL made, and OpenSSL checks ours.
- Kick tolerance now says when the open hole itself, not the shoe, is
  the limit.

Acceptance section DF (4 checks).
