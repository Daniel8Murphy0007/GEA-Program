v0.56.0 — COMPANY SIGN-IN

People can now sign in to the control panel with the account their
company already manages - Microsoft Entra ID, Okta, Google Workspace,
Ping, Keycloak or AD FS - so the company's multi-factor, its access rules
and its leavers apply to GEA too.

The sign-in is OpenID Connect with PKCE. The token the company's
provider signs is checked by the program itself with the standard
library: the signature against the provider's published keys, who issued
it, which application it is for, when it expires, and that it belongs to
this sign-in. The role in GEA comes from the app roles or groups the
company gives the person, through a map the administrator keeps; someone
with no mapped role does not get in, and the page says why. Local
accounts stay, for the first administrator and for when the provider
cannot be reached.

Administration has a Company sign-in card (set it, test the provider,
remove it), the sign-in page has the button, and `gea oidc` does the same
from a terminal. `gea help company-sign-in` walks through the Entra ID
app registration. The search box and Sign out button no longer show on
the sign-in page.

Section CY is new, four checks, run against a stand-in provider over
HTTPS. The gate is 467 checks. The standalone guard is clean.
