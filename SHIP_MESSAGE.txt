v0.69.0 — GEA READER, THE PROGRAM

GEA Reader is now a Windows program your clients can install.

- GEA-Reader-Setup-0.69.0.exe on the release: GEA Reader in the Start
  menu and on the desktop, with its own icon (a page with a green seal)
  and its own taskbar button - not Edge's. Right-click a dossier and
  choose "Check with GEA Reader" to check it at once.
- A client saves your site's fingerprint once; from then on every
  dossier you sign reads Valid with your name on it.
- Nothing of GEA-Program is installed with it, no administrator rights,
  no network. A portable zip of the same program is on the release too.

GEA-Program itself: install GEA-Program-Setup-0.69.0.exe over the old
one. C:\site is kept as it is.
