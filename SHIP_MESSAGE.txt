v0.100.0 — STICK-SLIP, BIT BOUNCE AND THE FRICTION FACTOR AT EACH CONNECTION

Added: stick-slip on the Drilling alerts tab. In two-minute blocks of
rotary drilling the surface torque's swing at periods of 3 to 15 seconds
is taken as a share of the torque; a block is flagged when the swing is
a tenth or more and one period carries it. The pipe rocked while
sliding, a swing at the rotary's own speed and a swing with no period
are set aside and counted.

Added: bit bounce - the hook load jumping from one second to the next
about its own level.

Added: the friction factor at each connection. The Torque & drag model,
with the string declared there, is asked at every connection what
open-hole factor makes the string weigh what the hook read. A rise of
0.05 over the connections before is flagged: the slow climb the
tight-hole rule lets pass.

Added: alerts drilling.stickslip, drilling.bounce, drilling.sticking,
and four settings, each changed with its source.

These are surface readings, once a second: a floor, not a measurement at
the bit. A rise in the friction factor is not a forecast of stuck pipe.

Changed: the standalone check turned away any tag with a three-digit
minor as another program's mark. GEA's own minor is now three digits; by
the owner's decision such a tag is turned away only when it is above
GEA's own version.
